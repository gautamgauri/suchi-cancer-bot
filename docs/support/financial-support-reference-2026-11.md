# Financial support for cancer treatment in India: verified reference, November 2026

This is source material for future knowledge base articles. It is not yet a knowledge base article and has not been ingested. It changes no behaviour.

Every fact below was read on an official page fetched on 25 November 2026. The source and the date sit beside each fact. Where something could not be confirmed on an official page, the entry says so and names the pages tried. Search results, news reports, hospital blogs and charity pages were used only to find official pages.

A machine-readable copy is in `financial-support-reference-2026-11.json`.

Schemes change. Before this material is turned into articles, each fact should be checked again against its source, and the "checked on" date updated.

## What a family should do first

1. Check whether the family is already covered by Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY. Log in with a mobile number at [beneficiary.nha.gov.in](https://beneficiary.nha.gov.in/) or call the toll-free helpline 14555. (Checked on 25 November 2026, [beneficiary.nha.gov.in](https://beneficiary.nha.gov.in/) and [pmjay.nha.gov.in/tollFree](https://pmjay.nha.gov.in/tollFree).)
2. If anyone in the family is aged 70 or over, they can get the Ayushman Vay Vandana card whatever the family's income. Aadhaar is the only document needed. (Checked on 25 November 2026, [senior citizen FAQ](https://pmjay.nha.gov.in/strapi/uploads/English_FA_Qs_related_to_the_benefits_for_senior_citizens_7596c43be0.pdf).)
3. Look up your own state's scheme in the state list below and note its helpline. Many states run their scheme together with PM-JAY.
4. At the hospital, carry a ration card or a government photo ID such as Aadhaar for PM-JAY. (Checked on 25 November 2026, [PM-JAY FAQ](https://pmjay.nha.gov.in/strapi/uploads/AB_PMJAY_FA_Qs_ed45d95556.pdf).)
5. If the patient's family lives below the poverty line and the treatment is at a Regional Cancer Centre, ask the hospital about the Health Minister's Cancer Patient Fund. The application goes through the treating government hospital.
6. When travelling by train for treatment, ask for the cancer patient concession. It also covers one escort.
7. Use only official websites and helplines. The National Health Authority warns of fraudulent portals, calls and messages about enrolment. (Checked on 25 November 2026, [nha.gov.in](https://nha.gov.in/).)

## 1. Ayushman Bharat Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (PM-JAY)

| Fact | Source | Checked on |
|---|---|---|
| PM-JAY gives a cover of Rs 5 lakh per family per year for secondary and tertiary care hospitalisation, at public and private empanelled hospitals across India. | [pmjay.nha.gov.in/about-ayushman-bharat](https://pmjay.nha.gov.in/about-ayushman-bharat) | 25 Nov 2026 |
| It is meant for over 12 crore poor and vulnerable families, about 55 crore people, the bottom 40% of the population. | [pmjay.nha.gov.in/about-ayushman-bharat](https://pmjay.nha.gov.in/about-ayushman-bharat) | 25 Nov 2026 |
| There is no limit on family size, age or gender. Pre-existing conditions are covered from the first day. Up to 3 days before admission and 15 days after discharge are covered. The benefit can be used anywhere in India. | [pmjay.nha.gov.in/about-ayushman-bharat](https://pmjay.nha.gov.in/about-ayushman-bharat) | 25 Nov 2026 |
| In rural areas, families qualify under the SECC 2011 deprivation criteria D1 to D5 and D7. In towns, families qualify under 11 occupational groups, such as domestic workers, street vendors, construction workers, sanitation workers and transport workers. States may use their own lists but must cover every family eligible under SECC. | [pmjay.nha.gov.in/criteria-coverage](https://pmjay.nha.gov.in/criteria-coverage) | 25 Nov 2026 |
| Treatment packages cover about 24 specialities, oncology among them. The package rate includes medicines, tests, food, and costs up to 15 days after discharge for the same illness. | [pmjay.nha.gov.in/PackagesAndRates](https://pmjay.nha.gov.in/PackagesAndRates) | 25 Nov 2026 |
| A family can check whether it is covered by logging in with a mobile number on the beneficiary portal. The portal also offers Aadhaar linking, adding a member, e-KYC and a status check. | [beneficiary.nha.gov.in](https://beneficiary.nha.gov.in/) | 25 Nov 2026 |
| Beneficiaries can now make their own Ayushman card with the Ayushman app. | [beneficiary.nha.gov.in](https://beneficiary.nha.gov.in/) | 25 Nov 2026 |
| At admission, carry a ration card or any government photo ID such as Aadhaar. | [PM-JAY FAQ (PDF)](https://pmjay.nha.gov.in/strapi/uploads/AB_PMJAY_FA_Qs_ed45d95556.pdf) | 25 Nov 2026 |
| Empanelled hospitals can be found through "Find Hospitals" on the NHA site, which leads to hem.nha.gov.in/search. | [nha.gov.in](https://nha.gov.in/) | 25 Nov 2026 |
| The PM-JAY toll-free helpline is 14555. | [pmjay.nha.gov.in/tollFree](https://pmjay.nha.gov.in/tollFree) | 25 Nov 2026 |
| nha.gov.in is the only official website of PM-JAY. The NHA warns of fraudulent portals, calls and messages about enrolment or premiums. | [nha.gov.in](https://nha.gov.in/) | 25 Nov 2026 |

### Ayushman Vay Vandana card, for people aged 70 and over

| Fact | Source | Checked on |
|---|---|---|
| Every citizen aged 70 or over, whatever the family's income, is eligible for free treatment up to Rs 5 lakh. Age is taken from Aadhaar. | [Senior citizen FAQ (PDF)](https://pmjay.nha.gov.in/strapi/uploads/English_FA_Qs_related_to_the_benefits_for_senior_citizens_7596c43be0.pdf) | 25 Nov 2026 |
| Aadhaar e-KYC is required and Aadhaar is the only document needed. A separate Ayushman card is issued. Apply on beneficiary.nha.gov.in or the Ayushman app. | Same FAQ | 25 Nov 2026 |
| The Rs 5 lakh is shared between eligible seniors in the same family. A senior whose family is already under PM-JAY gets an extra Rs 5 lakh for themselves. | Same FAQ | 25 Nov 2026 |
| Private insurance or ESIC cover does not stop a senior from joining. A person covered by CGHS or another government scheme must choose one, and the choice cannot be changed. | Same FAQ | 25 Nov 2026 |
| A missed call to 1800110770 brings details of the card. | [pmjay.nha.gov.in/tollFree](https://pmjay.nha.gov.in/tollFree) | 25 Nov 2026 |

### Health Minister's Cancer Patient Fund (within Rashtriya Arogya Nidhi)

| Fact | Source | Checked on |
|---|---|---|
| The fund gives financial help to poor patients living below the poverty line who have cancer, for treatment at 27 Regional Cancer Centres. It was set up in 2009. | [myscheme.gov.in/schemes/hmcpf](https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/hmcpf) | 25 Nov 2026 |
| Each Regional Cancer Centre holds a revolving fund. Up to Rs 5 lakh can be given towards treatment there. Cases needing more go to the Ministry. The most the scheme allows is Rs 15 lakh. | [myscheme.gov.in/schemes/hmcpf](https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/hmcpf) | 25 Nov 2026 |
| It covers radiation of all kinds, chemotherapy with supportive medicines, bone marrow transplant, tests including PET scans, and surgery for operable tumours. | [myscheme.gov.in/schemes/hmcpf](https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/hmcpf) | 25 Nov 2026 |
| Central and state government employees and public sector employees cannot apply. The grant is not given where treatment is free. | [myscheme.gov.in/schemes/hmcpf](https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/hmcpf) | 25 Nov 2026 |
| To apply, fill in the prescribed form, have it signed and stamped by the treating doctor or head of department and countersigned by the Medical Superintendent, and hand it in at that government hospital. Attach the original BPL or income certificate from the BDO, Tehsildar, SDM or a similar officer, and a copy of the ration card attested by a Gazetted Officer. | [myscheme.gov.in/schemes/hmcpf](https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/hmcpf) | 25 Nov 2026 |
| A Press Information Bureau backgrounder of February 2025 gives the same limits of Rs 5 lakh and Rs 15 lakh, and the 27 centres. | [PIB, Towards a Cancer-Free India (PDF)](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2025/feb/doc2025213500801.pdf) | 25 Nov 2026 |

|---|---|---|

| A concession needs the required certificate from the person or organisation concerned. | Same page | 25 Nov 2026 |
