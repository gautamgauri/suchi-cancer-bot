# Hospital directory contact verification, November 2026

This report records a check of every entry in `apps/landing/src/content/hospitals.json` against official sources. The checks ran on 24 and 25 November 2026. The machine-readable copy is `docs/navigator/hospital-verification-2026-11.json`. It holds the same facts keyed by id, with a source URL and date for every confirmed value.

A value counts as confirmed only where a page was fetched during this check and the value was seen on it. Search results were used to find official sites and were never used as the source of a value. No hospital was telephoned, emailed or messaged. Doctors, staff, costs, quality, waiting times and outcomes were outside the scope and were not examined.

In all, 77 values in 54 entries were changed in the directory file. Each change is listed in the JSON copy under `changes_applied`, with its source URL. Duplicates are shown in a separate column, so an entry can be both corrected and a duplicate.

How the statuses are used.

- `confirmed` means phone, address and cancer care were all seen on the official site and matched the record.
- `corrected` means an official page showed a different phone, address or website, and the record was changed to match.
- `corrected (partly confirmed)` means some values were corrected and at least one of phone, address or cancer care could not be confirmed.
- `partly confirmed` means nothing needed changing, but at least one of those values could not be confirmed.
- `not a cancer hospital per its own site` means the official site shows no oncology or cancer service.
- `could not confirm` means no official website was found.
- `site unreachable` means an official site exists but could not be read.

RCHRC Ranchi states that it "is now empanelled with PM-JAY MM-JAY" ([source](https://www.rchrc.org/ayushman-bharat-pm-jay-mm-jay), 2026-11-24). This agrees with the recorded value. AIIMS Deoghar publishes a circular about cancer patients availing benefits under AB-PMJAY ([source](https://www.aiimsdeoghar.edu.in/), 2026-11-24). Every other PM-JAY value in the file remains unconfirmed. `sctimst-thiruvananthapuram` and `nimhans-bangalore` are recorded as PM-JAY empanelled for cancer care. Nothing on their sites supports that.
- **Website fields.** Fifty-three entries have no `website` field. Adding one would change the file structure, so none was added. The official website found for each is in the JSON copy.
- **Certificates.** The sites of PGIMER Chandigarh, JIPMER and Kidwai (`kmio.karnataka.gov.in`) did not present a certificate that a standard client accepts on the dates checked. They loaded when the check was skipped. A patient's browser may show a security warning.
- **Group numbers.** Several private hospitals print one group-wide number against every unit. Medanta prints +91-880-000-1068, Paras prints 8080808069 and Max Saket prints +91 926 888 0303. These were used where they are printed against the hospital in question.

## Coverage

The directory is to serve India as a whole. Of the 36 states and union territories, 15 have no entry. The ten states are Andhra Pradesh, Arunachal Pradesh, Goa, Himachal Pradesh, Manipur, Mizoram, Nagaland, Punjab, Sikkim and Tripura. The five union territories are Andaman and Nicobar Islands, Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu, Jammu and Kashmir, Ladakh and Lakshadweep. No entries were added in this task.

## Method and its limits

- Each hospital's own website was found, often through a search, and then fetched directly. Values were read from the fetched page. Up to a few pages per hospital were read, usually the home page, the contact page and a department list. Requests went one at a time with an ordinary browser user agent.
- Several sites build their pages in JavaScript. For HBCH Muzaffarpur and AIIMS Kalyani, the address and phone were read from the site's own script bundle, which holds the footer and header text. This is noted against those values.
- Numbers are recorded as printed, then written as +91 followed by the national number without the leading zero. Toll-free numbers keep their 1800 form. Dashes inside printed values are shown as plain hyphens in this report.
- A number printed on a website was never dialled. A printed number can still be out of date, misprinted or unstaffed.
- Where a site printed several numbers, the general enquiry or OPD line was chosen and the others were recorded as alternatives. Some choices involve judgement and are noted in the JSON copy.
- An entry marked "none shown" for cancer care means that the pages read did not show it. It does not prove the hospital offers no cancer care.
- Government sources such as the National Cancer Grid list and the PM-JAY hospital finder were not used, apart from the Tata Memorial Centre's own pages for its units.
- Recorded values that matched in substance were left unchanged even where their wording or number format differed from the site.
- Doctor names, notes, risk flags, scores, departments, status and PM-JAY fields were left exactly as they were.
