## 1. Executive Summary

Suchi (Suchitra Cancer Bot) is a public-facing cancer information assistant built for SCCF. It provides non-diagnostic, knowledge-base-backed cancer information with safety guardrails, voice input/output, and feedback collection. The bot serves patients, caregivers, and the general public through a web chat interface, with WhatsApp as a planned future channel.

Suchi is **not** a diagnostic tool. It is structurally designed to be incapable of producing unsupported medical claims — a principle called "truth by architecture." Every medical claim must be grounded in verified knowledge base sources with inline citations, or the system abstains entirely.

| **Prescription Dosing** | "How much should I take?", "What dose?" | Safety rule auto-refusal |

## 5. User Personas

### 5.1 Patient (Pre-Diagnosis)
- **Need:** Understanding symptoms, screening options, what to expect
- **Context:** Anxious, possibly first encounter with cancer-related information
- **Bot behavior:** Empathetic tone, clear next steps, "Questions to Ask Your Doctor" lists, referral to providers

### 5.2 Patient (Post-Diagnosis)
- **Need:** Treatment overviews, side effect education, what to expect during treatment
- **Context:** Overwhelmed, seeking reliable information they can understand
- **Bot behavior:** Evidence-based information with citations, emotional support, caution against stopping treatment

### 5.3 Caregiver
- **Need:** How to support the patient, practical caregiving tips, self-care
- **Context:** May be family member (parent, spouse, child), emotionally invested
- **Bot behavior:** Recognize caregiver context, provide both patient-care and self-care guidance

### 5.4 General Public
- **Need:** Educational information about cancer prevention, risk factors, screening
- **Context:** Curious, researching, may be student/journalist/health worker
- **Bot behavior:** Factual, well-cited, educational tone

### 7.2 Content Ingestion Pipelines

| Pipeline | Source | Technology | Schedule |
|----------|--------|-----------|----------|
| NCI Ingestion | cancer.gov, NCIt thesaurus | Python (`scripts/nci-ingestion/`) | Monthly |
| YouTube Transcripts | SCCF OncoTalks channel | Python (`scripts/youtube-transcripts/`) | Monthly |
| Manual KB Files | SCCF medical team | Markdown files + manifest.json | As needed |

### 7.3 Embedding & Retrieval

- **Embedding Model:** Gemini Embedding (768-dimension vectors)
- **Vector Store:** PostgreSQL + pgvector extension
- **Search Strategy:** Hybrid — vector similarity search + keyword fallback
- **Retrieval Enhancements:** Query decomposition, query expansion, synonym matching, cross-lingual support, reranking

## 8. Safety & Trust Architecture

### 8.1 Core Principle: "Grounded or Silent"

Suchi may answer **only** when it can cite from the trusted knowledge base. Otherwise, it says it doesn't know and provides safe next steps.

### 8.2 Safety Layers

| Layer | Component | Description |
|-------|-----------|-------------|
| 1 | **Emergency Fast Path** | Detects severe symptoms → immediate "Call 112/emergency services" response |
| 2 | **Self-Harm Detection** | Crisis patterns → mental health resources (Vandrevala Foundation, iCall) |
| 3 | **Policy Rules** | 20+ rules covering diagnosis refusal, dosing refusal, misinformation, etc. |
| 4 | **Evidence Gate** | Validates KB evidence quality before allowing LLM to generate |
| 5 | **Citation Enforcement** | Post-LLM check: medical content with <2 citations is discarded and replaced with safe fallback |
| 6 | **Disclaimer Engine** | Appends medical disclaimers to all responses |

### 8.3 Evidence Requirements

| Query Type | Min Passages | Min Sources | Rationale |
|------------|-------------|-------------|-----------|
| Treatment | 2 | 2 | Multiple authoritative sources required |
| Side Effects | 2 | 1 | Single authoritative source sufficient |
| Prevention | 1 | 1 | Well-established information |
| Screening | 2 | 1 | Authoritative guidelines required |
| Caregiver | 1 | 1 | General guidance |
| Navigation | 1 | 1 | Resource information |

### 8.4 Abstention Policy

When evidence is insufficient, the bot returns a **SafeFallbackResponse**:
- Acknowledges the limitation
- Refers to healthcare provider
- Links to trusted organizations (NCI, WHO, ACS)
- Never includes medical content, generic health tips, or paraphrased KB content

## 9. Quality Assurance — Evaluation Framework

### 9.1 Test Suite Structure

| Category | Location | Focus |
|----------|----------|-------|
| Tier 1 | `eval/cases/tier1/` | Cancer-type-specific queries (breast, cervical, oral, colorectal, prostate) |
| General Info | `eval/cases/generalinfo/` | Cross-cancer and general health queries |
| Voice | `eval/cases/voice/` | Voice input/output quality |

### 9.2 Scoring Methodology

**Dual scoring system:**

1. **Deterministic Checks** — Automated, rule-based:
   - Disclaimer present
   - No prohibited diagnosis language
   - Required sections present (warning signs, tests, doctor questions)
   - Citation count meets minimums
   - Response length within bounds

2. **LLM Judge** — AI-scored with rubric:
   - Medical accuracy against KB
   - Completeness of information
   - Empathy and tone
   - Citation quality
   - Uses consensus voting (2/2 must agree)

### 9.3 Deployment Gates

Evaluation runs are integrated into the CI/CD pipeline via `cloudbuild.eval.yaml` and `cloudbuild.gated.yaml`. A gated deployment requires eval pass rates above threshold before promoting to production.

## 13. Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|--------|-----------|
| LLM generates unsupported medical claims | Low | Critical | Evidence gate + citation enforcement (structural, not behavioral) |
| High response latency (>30s) | Medium | High | LLM timeout caps (15s), time budget (25s), hidden LLM call elimination |
| Knowledge base becomes stale | Medium | Medium | Monthly ingestion pipelines, recency requirements per source tier |
| User misinterprets information as diagnosis | Medium | High | Consent gate, disclaimers on every response, refusal rules for diagnostic queries |
| LLM provider outage | Low | High | Multi-provider support (Gemini primary, DeepSeek fallback) |
