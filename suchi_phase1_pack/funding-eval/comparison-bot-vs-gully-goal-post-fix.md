### Monitoring and Evaluation Plan

Gully Goal will rigorously track participant progress and program effectiveness through a comprehensive monitoring and evaluation (M&E) plan, ensuring continuous improvement and accountability to [Funder Name]. Our internal digital dashboard will be updated fortnightly with center-level data, providing real-time insights into program implementation. Reporting will occur on a regular cadence: daily attendance records, fortnightly activity reports to the internal dashboard, monthly consolidated progress reports, quarterly narrative and financial reports to [Funder Name], and an annual impact report summarizing key outcomes and lessons learned. This multi-tiered approach ensures that we can adapt our strategies as needed to maximize impact for the 771 children and adolescent girls across Bihar participating in Gully Goal.

### Sustainability

## Sustainability

Gully Goal's enduring impact will stem from youth leadership, existing infrastructure, community ownership, diversified funding, and institutional learning. These five pillars will ensure the program's benefits extend far beyond the grant period, fostering a sustainable ecosystem for football-for-development in Bihar.

Our commitment to **youth-led sustainability** is embodied in our Young Leader pipeline. We will train 9 Young Leaders from the communities we serve as Football3 coaches and mediators. These Young Leaders will undergo intensive training in Football3 methodology, child safeguarding, and facilitation skills. Over the grant period, they will progressively co-facilitate sessions with experienced coaches, culminating in them independently leading sessions by the end of Year 2. We aim to retain at least 75% of trained Young Leaders as active coaches and mentors in the years following the grant, providing them with continued professional development opportunities.

Gully Goal will leverage **existing infrastructure** to minimize costs and maximize reach. No new facilities require construction; we will utilize community playing spaces and school grounds already accessible to our target communities. Our 3 existing KHEL centers in Patna, Bihta, and Sarairanjan will serve as hubs for program activities, providing safe and familiar environments for participants. The equipment required for Football3 is reusable and low-cost, ensuring long-term affordability. Furthermore, we will integrate Gully Goal with our Empowering Futures partner-school network, creating synergies and shared resources.

**Community ownership** is paramount to Gully Goal's sustainability. We will actively engage families and community members through regular parent meetings, workshops, and community football festivals. These events will showcase the positive impact of the program on children's development and foster a sense of collective responsibility. We will emphasize the link between sports and education, addressing the "study vs. play" cultural barrier by demonstrating how Football3 enhances teamwork, problem-solving, and leadership skills – all valuable assets for academic success.

Diksha Foundation has a **diversified funding base** that we will continue to cultivate. We currently receive support from organizations such as the Azim Premji Foundation and Feeding India. To ensure the long-term financial sustainability of Gully Goal, we will actively pursue CSR partnerships, leveraging our CSR-1 registration to attract funding from companies aligned with our mission. We will also explore opportunities to align Gully Goal with relevant government schemes, such as the Bihar Khel Niti and Mukhyamantri Khel Vikas Yojana (if sports-related), to secure additional resources.

Finally, we are committed to **institutional learning** and knowledge sharing. Diksha Foundation utilizes a centralized MIS to collect and analyze program data, which is visualized through data dashboards for real-time monitoring and decision-making. We will meticulously document the Gully Goal model, including training materials, session plans, and best practices. This documentation will be aligned with our internal capability framework, enabling us to replicate and scale the program effectively in other communities.
