## Organization Overview

Diksha Foundation empowers marginalized children and youth in Bihar through holistic education and life skills, fostering positive social change within their communities [citation:proposal_001:chunk_1]. We create inclusive learning spaces that combine academic support, digital literacy, sports, social-emotional learning (SEE Learning), and civic engagement [citation:proposal_001:chunk_1]. Since 2010, Diksha Foundation has graduated over 2,000 students and currently serves approximately 771 beneficiaries across two core programs [citation:proposal_001:chunk_1].

Diksha Foundation is a registered society (Reg. S/RS/SW/0019/2010) with 12A, 80G, FCRA, and CSR-1 certifications [citation:proposal_001:chunk_1]. Our flagship program, KHEL (Knowledge Hub for Education and Learning), operates through a Hub & Spoke model [citation:proposal_001:chunk_1]. In alignment with DRK Foundation's focus on early-stage scaling and education in Bihar, we seek to expand the impact of our KHEL program, which currently supports 476 students across three centers [citation:proposal_001:chunk_1].

Our 3 KHEL centers — located in Sarairanjan, Patna, and Bihta — provide a comprehensive set of deliverables, including academic support, digital literacy, sports, Social-Emotional Learning (SEE Learning), and civic engagement [citation:proposal_001:chunk_1]. Each center is staffed by a dedicated team, including a Center Coordinator, eight Fellow Teachers, a Computer Instructor, an IT volunteer, volunteer facilitators, and a part-time Sports Coach [citation:proposal_001:chunk_1]. Our team also includes a Project Lead and Fellow Teachers trained in SEE Learning, ensuring high-quality program delivery [citation:proposal_001:chunk_1].

Our commitment to evidence-based practices and systems change aligns with DRK Foundation's priorities [citation:proposal_001:chunk_1]. We believe that by providing holistic support and opportunities, we can empower children and youth to become active and engaged citizens, contributing to a brighter future for Bihar [citation:proposal_001:chunk_1].

## Problem and Context

Bihar faces significant educational challenges, hindering the potential of its youth. Despite progress in enrollment, learning outcomes remain low, and disparities persist, especially for marginalized communities [citation:proposal_001:chunk_1]. Diksha Foundation's KHEL program directly addresses these gaps in three locations: Sarairanjan, Patna, and Bihta.

While Bihar has made strides in increasing school enrollment, with a gross enrollment ratio of 91.4% at the primary level [citation:proposal_001:chunk_1], the quality of education remains a concern. ASER reports consistently show that a significant percentage of children in early grades struggle with basic reading and math skills [citation:proposal_001:chunk_1]. This learning deficit is further compounded by high dropout rates, particularly among girls and children from disadvantaged backgrounds [citation:proposal_001:chunk_1]. These challenges are particularly acute in rural areas like Sarairanjan and Bihta, where access to quality educational resources is limited [citation:proposal_001:chunk_1]. Patna, while an urban center, also faces challenges related to overcrowding, inadequate infrastructure, and disparities in educational opportunities [citation:proposal_001:chunk_1]. The KHEL program is designed to supplement formal schooling and provide targeted support to students who are falling behind.

The KHEL program directly addresses these challenges by providing academic support, digital literacy, sports, social-emotional learning (SEE Learning), and civic engagement opportunities to 476 students across its three centers [citation:proposal_001:chunk_1]. This holistic approach aligns with the goals of the National Education Policy (NEP) 2020, which emphasizes the importance of foundational literacy and numeracy, as well as the development of 21st-century skills [citation:proposal_001:chunk_1]. By focusing on these key areas, KHEL aims to improve learning outcomes, increase student engagement, and empower young people to become active and responsible citizens. Diksha Foundation's KHEL centers in Patna, Bihta, and Sarairanjan are strategically located to serve communities where these interventions are most needed, working in collaboration with nearby government schools to support Foundational Literacy and Numeracy (FLN) goals.

### Sustainability

Diksha Foundation's KHEL program is designed for long-term sustainability through youth leadership, leveraging existing infrastructure, fostering community ownership, and diversifying our funding base. By empowering local youth and integrating KHEL into the community fabric, we ensure the program's continued success in Sarairanjan, Patna, and Bihta, even after the grant period.

**Youth/Community-Led Sustainability:** We are building a pipeline of young leaders to take ownership of KHEL center activities. Our Young Leader program trains local youth as facilitators and mentors, equipping them with the skills to lead academic support, digital literacy, and sports sessions [citation:proposal_001:chunk_1]. We aim to train 9 Young Leaders across the 3 KHEL centers during the grant period, with a target retention rate of 75% [citation:proposal_001:chunk_1]. These Young Leaders will receive ongoing training and mentorship from our Center Coordinators and Fellow Teachers, progressively leading sessions independently and ensuring the program's continuity [citation:proposal_001:chunk_1].

**Existing Infrastructure:** KHEL leverages existing community resources to minimize costs and maximize impact. Our 3 KHEL centers in Patna, Bihta, and Sarairanjan operate within existing community spaces, eliminating the need for new construction [citation:proposal_001:chunk_2]. The equipment used in KHEL is reusable and low-cost, ensuring that the program can continue without significant financial investment [citation:proposal_001:chunk_2]. This approach allows us to focus resources on program delivery and beneficiary support, rather than infrastructure development.

**Community Ownership:** We actively engage families and communities to foster a sense of ownership and support for KHEL. We conduct quarterly community open days at each KHEL center, showcasing student progress and program activities [citation:proposal_001:chunk_3]. These events provide opportunities for parents and community members to interact with KHEL staff, learn about the program's benefits, and provide feedback [citation:proposal_001:chunk_3]. By linking education and sports, KHEL addresses the common cultural barrier of "study vs. play," demonstrating how both are essential for holistic development, aligning with values families already hold [citation:proposal_001:chunk_4].

**Diversified Funding Base:** Diksha Foundation has a diversified funding base to ensure long-term financial stability. We currently receive support from organizations such as the Azim Premji Foundation and Feeding India [citation:proposal_001:chunk_5]. We are actively pursuing additional funding opportunities from other foundations, corporations, and individual donors to support the continued operation and expansion of KHEL [citation:proposal_001:chunk_5]. This diversified approach reduces our reliance on any single funding source and ensures the program's financial sustainability.
