## Proposed Program: Empowering Marginalized Children Through KHEL

Diksha Foundation proposes to expand our KHEL (Knowledge Hub for Education and Learning) program, directly benefiting 476 students across our three centers in Bihar: Sarairanjan, Patna, and Bihta [citation:proposal_001:chunk_3]. This initiative aligns with RIST Trust's focus on education equity, community development, and adolescent empowerment in Bihar by providing holistic education and life skills to marginalized children [citation:proposal_001:chunk_3]. Our approach creates inclusive learning spaces that combine academic support, digital literacy, sports, social-emotional learning (SEE Learning), and civic engagement, equipping children with the skills and confidence to succeed in education, employment, and life.

Our KHEL program operates through a Hub & Spoke model, with three centers strategically located to serve marginalized communities [citation:proposal_001:chunk_3]. These centers provide supplementary education, ICT labs, access to Khan Academy, SEE Learning, and sports activities [citation:proposal_001:chunk_3]. We will leverage the existing infrastructure and expertise at KHEL Patna (Rukanpura), KHEL Bihta (Sikaria), and KHEL Sarairanjan to deliver targeted interventions that address the specific needs of each community [citation:proposal_001:chunk_3]. Our team includes 3 Center Coordinators, 8 Fellow Teachers (trained in SEE Learning), a Computer Instructor, an IT volunteer, Volunteer facilitators, a Project Lead, an External Sports Trainer, and a part-time Sports Coach, ensuring comprehensive support for our students [citation:proposal_001:chunk_3].

*Organizational Context:* Diksha Foundation is a registered society (Reg. S/RS/SW/0019/2010) established in 2010 in Bihar, India, with 12A, 80G, FCRA, and CSR-1 certifications. Since inception, we have graduated over 2,000 students through our KHEL Centers and currently serve approximately 771 beneficiaries across two core programs: KHEL and Empowering Futures. Our Empowering Futures program reaches 260 adolescent girls [citation:proposal_001:chunk_3].

## Monitoring and Evaluation

Our robust monitoring and evaluation (M&E) framework will ensure KHEL [Knowledge Hub for Education and Learning] effectively reaches its goal of providing quality education to 476 students across our three centers in Sarairanjan, Patna, and Bihta [citation:proposal_001:chunk_1]. This framework will provide continuous data to inform program improvements and demonstrate impact to RIST Trust, aligning with their focus on education equity and community development in Bihar [citation:proposal_001:chunk_2].

Our M&E system incorporates regular data collection, analysis, and reporting at multiple levels. Fellow Teachers will maintain daily attendance registers and conduct ongoing classroom assessments [citation:proposal_001:chunk_3]. Center Coordinators will consolidate this data into fortnightly progress reports, which are then fed into our internal digital dashboard [citation:proposal_001:chunk_3]. The M&E Officer will conduct monthly data verification to ensure accuracy, while the Project Lead will perform quarterly spot-checks [citation:proposal_001:chunk_3]. We will conduct baseline assessments in Month 1, midline assessments in Month 6, and endline assessments in Month 12 to measure progress against key indicators [citation:proposal_001:chunk_3]. These indicators include attendance, literacy/numeracy gains, digital literacy skills, sports participation, and social-emotional learning (SEL) competencies [citation:proposal_001:chunk_3]. Data will be collected using tools such as ASER-style assessments, digital literacy practical tests, sports participation logs, and SEL observation checklists [citation:proposal_001:chunk_3].

Reporting will occur at multiple intervals to ensure transparency and accountability. We will provide daily attendance records, fortnightly activity reports to our internal dashboard, monthly consolidated progress reports, quarterly narrative and financial reports to RIST Trust, and an annual impact report [citation:proposal_001:chunk_3]. This multi-tiered approach will allow us to track progress, identify challenges, and make data-driven decisions to optimize the KHEL program for the 476 students in Sarairanjan, Patna, and Bihta [citation:proposal_001:chunk1].

### Sustainability

KHEL's impact on 476 students across Sarairanjan, Patna, and Bihta will endure through youth leadership, leveraging existing infrastructure, fostering community ownership, and diversifying our funding base.

Our youth-led sustainability model centers on cultivating Young Leaders from within the KHEL program. We will train 9 Young Leaders as sports coaches and mentors, equipping them with the skills to progressively lead KHEL sessions independently [citation:proposal_001:chunk_10]. These Young Leaders will undergo a structured training program, including mentorship from experienced coaches and participation in SEE Learning workshops, ensuring they are well-prepared to guide and inspire younger participants [citation:proposal_001:chunk_10]. Our goal is to retain at least 75% of trained Young Leaders in active roles within the KHEL centers for at least one year post-grant, creating a sustainable pipeline of local talent [citation:proposal_001:chunk_10].

KHEL leverages existing infrastructure to minimize reliance on new resources. No new facilities require construction, and equipment is reusable and low-cost [citation:proposal_001:chunk_11]. Our 3 KHEL centers in Patna, Bihta, and Sarairanjan will continue to serve as hubs for learning and development, providing a stable base for program activities [citation:proposal_001:chunk_11]. Furthermore, we will continue to utilize community playing spaces for sports activities, fostering a sense of shared ownership and responsibility [citation:proposal_001:chunk_11].

Community ownership is crucial for long-term sustainability. We actively engage parents through regular meetings and workshops, highlighting the benefits of the KHEL program and encouraging their involvement in their children's learning journey [citation:proposal_001:chunk_12]. We also organize quarterly community events, such as sports tournaments and cultural performances, to showcase the talents of KHEL participants and build community support [citation:proposal_001:chunk_12]. By linking education and sports, we address the common "study vs. play" cultural barrier, demonstrating how both can contribute to a child's holistic development [citation:proposal_001:chunk_12].

To ensure financial sustainability, we are actively diversifying our funding base. We have established partnerships with organizations such as the Azim Premji Foundation and Feeding India [citation:proposal_001:chunk_13]. We are also exploring CSR partnerships, leveraging our CSR-1 registration to attract funding from corporations committed to social impact [citation:proposal_001:chunk_13]. Furthermore, we will align our program with relevant government schemes, such as the Bihar Khel Niti and Mukhyamantri Khel Vikas Yojna, to access additional funding and resources [citation:proposal_001:chunk_13].
