## Executive Summary

Diksha Foundation proposes the "Holistic Education and Life Skills Program" to empower 476 students across three KHEL centers in Bihar: Sarairanjan, Patna, and Bihta [citation:proposal_001:chunk_3]. This initiative aligns with HCL Foundation's focus on education quality, life skills, holistic development, and adolescent girls, equipping marginalized children with essential skills for success in education, employment, and personal development [citation:proposal_001:chunk_3].

Our program delivers daily academic support, weekly digital literacy training, sports for development, Social-Emotional Learning (SEE Learning), monthly civic engagement projects, life skills workshops, and a dedicated adolescent girls empowerment program [citation:proposal_001:chunk_3]. These activities create inclusive learning spaces that address the holistic needs of children and youth in marginalized communities [citation:proposal_001:chunk_3]. We leverage sports to teach teamwork, discipline, and leadership, while SEE Learning fosters self-awareness, empathy, and responsible decision-making [citation:proposal_001:chunk_3].

The program will be implemented by a dedicated team including 8 Fellow Teachers, 3 Center Coordinators, a Computer Instructor, an IT volunteer, volunteer facilitators, a Project Lead, an External Sports Trainer, and a part-time Sports Coach [citation:proposal_001:chunk_3]. Diksha Foundation is a registered society (Reg. S/RS/SW/0019/2010) established in 2010 in Bihar, India, with 12A, 80G, FCRA, and CSR-1 certifications [citation:proposal_001:chunk_3]. Since inception, we have graduated over 2,000 students through our KHEL Centers and currently serve approximately 771 beneficiaries [citation:proposal_001:chunk_3]. The "Holistic Education and Life Skills Program" builds upon this foundation, aiming to create lasting positive change in the lives of vulnerable children and adolescents in Bihar.

## Problem Statement

Bihar faces significant challenges in ensuring equitable access to quality education and holistic development for its children, particularly in rural communities. Our Holistic Education and Life Skills Program directly addresses these gaps by providing comprehensive support to 476 students across our three KHEL centers in Sarairanjan, Patna, and Bihta.

Despite progress in enrollment, Bihar lags behind national averages in key education indicators. While the state has made strides in increasing school enrollment, dropout rates remain a concern, particularly at the secondary level [citation:proposal_001:chunk_1]. Learning outcomes are also significantly below national benchmarks, with a substantial proportion of students in elementary grades struggling with foundational literacy and numeracy skills [citation:proposal_001:chunk_1]. These disparities are further exacerbated for children from marginalized communities and adolescent girls, who often face additional barriers to accessing quality education and life skills development [citation:proposal_001:chunk_2]. Existing educational programs often lack a holistic approach, focusing primarily on academic performance while neglecting crucial aspects of social-emotional learning, digital literacy, and life skills [citation:proposal_001:chunk_3]. This gap hinders the overall development of children and limits their future opportunities.

Our intervention directly tackles these challenges by providing a comprehensive program that integrates academic support, digital literacy training, sports for development, social-emotional learning (SEE Learning), civic engagement projects, life skills workshops, and an adolescent girls empowerment program [citation:proposal_001:chunk_3]. By addressing these interconnected needs, we aim to equip students with the skills and knowledge necessary to succeed academically, develop essential life skills, and become active and engaged citizens. This approach aligns with the goals of the National Education Policy (NEP) 2020, which emphasizes holistic development and the integration of life skills into the curriculum [citation:proposal_001:chunk_4]. Our program is designed to complement existing educational efforts in Bihar, providing targeted support to students who need it most and empowering them to reach their full potential.

## Monitoring and Evaluation

Our robust Monitoring and Evaluation (M&E) framework will ensure the Holistic Education and Life Skills Program effectively reaches its target of 476 students across our three KHEL centers in Sarairanjan, Patna, and Bihta. We will use a mixed-methods approach to track progress, identify areas for improvement, and demonstrate the program's impact on students' academic performance, life skills, and overall well-being.

Our M&E system incorporates continuous data collection and analysis at multiple levels. Fellow Teachers will maintain daily attendance registers and conduct regular ASER-style literacy and numeracy assessments to monitor academic progress [citation:proposal_001:chunk_3]. Digital literacy will be assessed through practical tests, while sports participation logs and SEL observation checklists will track engagement and development in these areas [citation:proposal_001:chunk_3]. Center Coordinators will consolidate this data into fortnightly progress reports, providing a real-time view of program implementation across the three KHEL centers [citation:proposal_001:chunk_3]. The Project Lead will conduct quarterly reviews of the data to identify trends, challenges, and areas for program adaptation [citation:proposal_001:chunk_3].

To measure program impact over time, we will conduct baseline, midline, and endline assessments. The baseline will be conducted in Month 1, the midline in Month 6, and the endline in Month 12 [citation:proposal_001:chunk_3]. This data will be collected and analyzed by our M&E Officer, who will also be responsible for generating monthly consolidated progress reports and quarterly narrative and financial reports for HCL Foundation [citation:proposal_001:chunk_3]. ## Sustainability

The Holistic Education and Life Skills Program will transition to community ownership by cultivating local leadership and leveraging existing resources, ensuring its long-term viability for the 476 students across our 3 KHEL centers [citation:proposal_001:chunk_1]. We will achieve this through a multi-pronged approach focused on youth leadership, infrastructure utilization, community ownership, government integration, and financial diversification.

**1. Youth/Community-Led Sustainability:** We are committed to developing a pipeline of young leaders who will progressively take ownership of program delivery. We will recruit and train 9 Young Leaders from the communities surrounding our KHEL centers in Patna, Bihta, and Sarairanjan to serve as mediators and sports coaches [citation:proposal_001:chunk_2]. These Young Leaders will undergo intensive training in sports for development, social-emotional learning, and life skills facilitation, mentored by our experienced Fellow Teachers [citation:proposal_001:chunk_3]. Our goal is to retain at least 75% of these Young Leaders for at least one year post-grant, enabling them to independently lead sessions and mentor future cohorts, ensuring the program's continued impact [citation:proposal_001:chunk_4].

**2. Existing Infrastructure:** The program will leverage Diksha Foundation's established infrastructure to minimize reliance on new funding. Our 3 KHEL centers in Patna, Bihta, and Sarairanjan provide safe and accessible learning spaces, equipped with reusable and low-cost equipment [citation:proposal_001:chunk_5]. No new facilities require construction [citation:proposal_001:chunk_5]. These centers will continue to serve as hubs for academic support, digital literacy training, and life skills workshops, ensuring continuity for the 476 students [citation:proposal_001:chunk_1].

**3. Community Ownership:** We will foster a sense of ownership among families and communities through active engagement and participation. We will organize monthly community events at each KHEL center to showcase student progress, celebrate achievements, and solicit feedback on program design [citation:proposal_001:chunk_6]. These events will reinforce the value of education and life skills development, aligning program activities with the aspirations of families in Bihar [citation:proposal_001:chunk_7]. Furthermore, we will establish parent-teacher associations at each center to facilitate regular communication and collaborative problem-solving, strengthening the connection between the program and the community it serves [citation:proposal_001:chunk_8].
