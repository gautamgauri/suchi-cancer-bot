## Organization Overview

Diksha Foundation empowers marginalized children in Bihar through holistic education and life skills, creating inclusive learning spaces that combine academic support, digital literacy, sports, social-emotional learning (SEE Learning), and civic engagement. Since 2010, we have graduated over 2,000 students and currently serve approximately 771 beneficiaries across our programs [citation:proposal_001:chunk_1]. Diksha Foundation is a registered society (Reg. S/RS/SW/0019/2010) with 12A, 80G, FCRA, and CSR-1 certifications [citation:proposal_001:chunk_1].

Our flagship KHEL (Knowledge Hub for Education and Learning) program directly addresses the need for supplementary education and holistic development among underprivileged children in Bihar [citation:proposal_001:chunk_1]. KHEL operates through a Hub & Spoke model [citation:proposal_001:chunk_1]. Through KHEL, we aim to provide 476 students across three centers — KHEL Patna (Rukanpura), KHEL Danapur (Danapur Nizamat), and KHEL Phulwari (Phulwari Sharif) — with daily supplementary education, weekly ICT lab sessions and sports activities, weekly Social-Emotional Learning (SEE Learning) sessions, and monthly civic engagement activities [citation:proposal_001:chunk_1].

Our team comprises dedicated and qualified individuals committed to providing quality education and support to our students. This includes 8 Fellow Teachers, 3 Center Coordinators, a Computer Instructor, an IT volunteer, volunteer facilitators, a Project Lead, an External Sports Trainer, and a part-time Sports Coach [citation:proposal_001:chunk_1]. We also have Fellow Teachers trained in SEE Learning [citation:proposal_001:chunk_1]. In alignment with Asha for Education's focus on foundational learning and life skills (C4, C6), KHEL integrates academic support with activities designed to foster social-emotional development and civic responsibility, empowering children to become resilient and engaged members of their communities [citation:proposal_001:chunk_1].

## Problem Statement

Bihar faces significant challenges in ensuring equitable access to quality education, particularly for underprivileged children, hindering the achievement of Sustainable Development Goal 4 and the aims of the National Education Policy (NEP) 2020. While enrollment rates have improved, significant learning gaps persist, especially in foundational literacy and numeracy (FLN), compounded by socioeconomic disparities [citation:proposal_004:chunk_1]. Our KHEL (Knowledge Hub for Education and Learning) program directly addresses these gaps by providing targeted supplementary education and life skills development to 476 students across three centers: KHEL Patna (Rukanpura), KHEL Danapur (Danapur Nizamat), and KHEL Phulwari (Phulwari Sharif).

Despite progress in enrollment, dropout rates remain a concern, particularly among girls, further exacerbated by poverty and social barriers [citation:proposal_004:chunk_1]. According to ASER reports, a significant percentage of children in Bihar still lack basic reading and arithmetic skills appropriate for their grade level [citation:proposal_004:chunk_1]. These challenges are particularly acute in marginalized communities within Patna district, where our KHEL centers are located, limiting future opportunities for children from these communities. The KHEL program provides a safe and supportive learning environment, incorporating social-emotional learning (SEE Learning) and civic engagement activities to address these challenges and promote holistic development.

Existing educational interventions often lack the resources and capacity to provide individualized attention and comprehensive support needed to bridge these learning gaps [citation:proposal_004:chunk_1]. Our KHEL program directly addresses this gap by offering daily supplementary education, weekly ICT lab sessions, sports activities, and SEE Learning sessions, delivered by 8 Fellow Teachers and 3 Center Coordinators, along with support from a Computer Instructor, IT volunteer, Volunteer facilitators, Project Lead, External Sports Trainer, and part-time Sports Coach [citation:proposal_004:chunk_2]. This comprehensive approach aims to improve learning outcomes, enhance life skills, and empower children to succeed academically and personally, aligning with Asha for Education's focus on foundational learning and life skills for underprivileged children.

## Outcomes and Evaluation

KHEL will empower 476 students across three centers in Bihar with improved academic performance, digital literacy, and social-emotional skills, measured through a rigorous monitoring and evaluation framework. We will track progress at our KHEL Patna (Rukanpura), KHEL Danapur (Danapur Nizamat), and KHEL Phulwari (Phulwari Sharif) centers to ensure effective program delivery and impact [citation:proposal_001:chunk_14]. Our M&E framework includes baseline, midline, and endline assessments to measure student growth throughout the 12-month project period, with baseline assessments conducted in Month 1, midline in Month 6, and endline in Month 12 [citation:proposal_001:chunk_14].

Data collection will be comprehensive and multifaceted. Fellow Teachers will maintain daily attendance registers and conduct regular classroom assessments using ASER-style literacy and numeracy tests [citation:proposal_001:chunk_14]. Digital literacy will be assessed through practical tests in the ICT labs, while sports participation will be tracked via dedicated logs [citation:proposal_001:chunk_14]. Social-emotional learning (SEL) progress will be monitored using observation checklists, ensuring a holistic view of student development [citation:proposal_001:chunk_14]. Center Coordinators will consolidate fortnightly progress reports, providing a regular snapshot of center-level performance [citation:proposal_001:chunk_14].

Our reporting cadence ensures continuous monitoring and improvement. Daily attendance records are maintained by Fellow Teachers, with fortnightly activity reports submitted to an internal dashboard [citation:proposal_001:chunk_14]. Monthly consolidated progress reports will be generated, followed by quarterly narrative and financial reports to Asha for Education [citation:proposal_001:chunk_14]. An annual impact report will summarize the overall project outcomes [citation:proposal_001:chunk_14]. The M&E Officer will conduct monthly data verification, and the Project Lead will perform quarterly spot-checks to ensure data quality [citation:proposal_001:chunk_14].

### Sustainability

KHEL's sustainability is built on empowering local youth, leveraging existing resources, fostering community ownership, and diversifying funding sources. Our approach ensures that the KHEL program continues to benefit 476 students across our three centers — KHEL Patna (Rukanpura), KHEL Danapur (Danapur Nizamat), and KHEL Phulwari (Phulwari Sharif) — even after the grant period.

**1. Youth/Community-Led Sustainability:** We are committed to developing local leadership to ensure long-term program sustainability. We train 9 Young Leaders across the three KHEL centers to serve as mediators and coaches, progressively leading sessions independently [citation:proposal_001:chunk_1]. These Young Leaders receive comprehensive training in areas such as conflict resolution, sports coaching, and digital literacy, equipping them with the skills necessary to facilitate KHEL activities [citation:proposal_001:chunk_1]. Our goal is to retain at least 75% of these trained Young Leaders in a volunteer capacity for at least one year post-grant, ensuring a continued presence and leadership within the KHEL centers [citation:proposal_001:chunk_1].

**2. Existing Infrastructure:** KHEL leverages existing infrastructure to minimize costs and ensure continuity. Our 3 KHEL centers in Patna, Danapur, and Phulwari Sharif provide safe and accessible learning spaces for the 476 students [citation:proposal_001:chunk_2]. No new facilities require construction, and equipment is reusable and low-cost [citation:proposal_001:chunk_2]. The KHEL centers are equipped with ICT labs that will continue to be utilized for digital literacy sessions, even after the grant concludes [citation:proposal_001:chunk_2].

**3. Community Ownership:** We actively engage families and communities to foster a sense of ownership and investment in the KHEL program. We organize monthly community events at each of the 3 KHEL centers to showcase student progress, celebrate achievements, and gather feedback [citation:proposal_001:chunk_3]. These events not only build legitimacy for the program but also strengthen the bond between the KHEL centers and the communities they serve [citation:proposal_001:chunk_3]. By integrating sports activities with academic learning, we address the common cultural barrier of "study vs. play," demonstrating how both can contribute to a child's holistic development, aligning with values families already hold [citation:proposal_001:chunk_3].

**4. Diversified Funding:** To ensure the long-term financial sustainability of KHEL, we are actively pursuing diversified funding streams. We are developing relationships with local businesses and philanthropists to secure ongoing financial support for the KHEL centers [citation:proposal_001:chunk_4]. We also plan to explore opportunities for revenue generation through community-based initiatives, such as offering computer literacy classes to adults in the community [citation:proposal_001:chunk_4]. Our team will also apply for additional grants from organizations that support education and youth development in Bihar [citation:proposal_001:chunk_4].
