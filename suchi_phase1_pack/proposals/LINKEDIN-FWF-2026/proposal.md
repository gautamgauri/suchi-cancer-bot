## Organization Overview

Diksha Foundation empowers marginalized children and youth in Bihar with the skills and confidence to thrive in education and future employment. We create inclusive learning spaces that combine academic support, digital literacy, sports, social-emotional learning (SEE Learning), and civic engagement, aligning with LinkedIn's Future of Work Fund 2026 priorities [citation:proposal_001:chunk_1]. Since 2010, we have graduated over 2,000 students and currently serve approximately 771 beneficiaries across our core programs [citation:proposal_001:chunk_1].

Diksha Foundation is a registered society (Reg. S/RS/SW/0019/2010) in Bihar, India, holding 12A, 80G, FCRA, and CSR-1 certifications [citation:proposal_001:chunk_1]. Our flagship program, KHEL (Knowledge Hub for Education and Learning), operates through a Hub & Spoke model across 3 centers: KHEL Patna (Rukanpura), KHEL Bihta (Sita Ram Ashram), and KHEL Sarairanjan [citation:proposal_001:chunk_1]. These centers serve 476 students with supplementary education, ICT labs, Khan Academy resources, SEE Learning, and sports activities [citation:proposal_001:chunk_1].

Our KHEL centers are staffed by dedicated teams, including 3 Center Coordinators, 8 Fellow Teachers (trained in SEE Learning), Computer Instructors, IT volunteers, volunteer facilitators, a Project Lead, an External Sports Trainer, and a part-time Sports Coach [citation:proposal_001:chunk_1]. This team ensures the effective delivery of our holistic curriculum, fostering both academic excellence and crucial life skills [citation:proposal_001:chunk_1]. Through KHEL, we aim to equip students with the digital skills, AI fluency, and workforce readiness necessary to succeed in the evolving landscape of work, aligning with LinkedIn's focus on technology education and youth employment [citation:proposal_001:chunk_1].

## Program Design and Delivery

If 476 students across our three KHEL centers in Bihar are provided with targeted AI fluency, digital literacy, and workforce readiness skills through a blended learning model incorporating SEE Learning and project-based activities, then they will develop enhanced problem-solving abilities, increased self-awareness, and improved employability prospects, leading to greater economic empowerment and participation in the future workforce. This program is designed to equip young learners with the tools they need to thrive in an evolving job market.

Our Theory of Change is structured as follows:

*   **Inputs:** The program leverages resources including 3 KHEL centers (Sarairanjan, Patna, and Bihta), 8 Fellow Teachers, 3 Center Coordinators, a dedicated Computer Instructor, IT volunteers, volunteer facilitators, a Project Lead, and a part-time Sports Coach. We will also provide digital devices, internet connectivity, learning materials, and access to online learning platforms.
*   **Activities:** We will conduct regular digital literacy classes, AI fluency workshops, and career counseling sessions. Students will participate in project-based learning activities, skill-building exercises, and guest lectures from industry professionals. Our team will train 8 Fellow Teachers in SEE Learning to integrate social-emotional skills into the curriculum.
*   **Outputs:** We expect to deliver 100+ hours of digital literacy training per student, conduct 12 AI fluency workshops across the three centers, and facilitate 6 career counseling sessions per center. Students will complete at least 2 project-based learning assignments and participate in quarterly community engagement events.
*   **Outcomes:** Participants will demonstrate improved digital literacy skills, enhanced problem-solving abilities, increased self-awareness, and a greater understanding of career pathways. We anticipate a 20% increase in students' confidence in using technology for learning and work (to be confirmed via pre- and post-program surveys).
*   **Impact:** The program aims to contribute to greater economic empowerment for 476 students by equipping them with the skills needed to succeed in the future workforce. We envision increased participation in higher education and vocational training programs, established to improved employment opportunities and long-term financial stability.

Our program integrates several evidence-based methodologies, adapted for the Bihar context and our hub-and-spoke model. We use **Project-Based Learning (PBL)**, where students learn by actively engaging in real-world and personally meaningful projects. PBL fosters critical thinking, problem-solving, and collaboration skills [citation:proposal_001:chunk_3]. In our KHEL centers, students will work on projects that address local challenges, such as developing a digital literacy campaign for their community or creating a mobile app to improve access to local resources. We are also implementing **Social, Emotional, and Ethical (SEE) Learning**, a K-12 education program developed by Emory University, which cultivates critical thinking, attention training, self-awareness, compassion, and ethical decision-making [citation:proposal_001:chunk_3]. SEE Learning is particularly relevant in the Bihar context, where many students face socio-economic challenges that can impact their well-being and academic performance. Our 8 Fellow Teachers will be trained in SEE Learning to integrate these principles into their teaching practices, creating a more supportive and inclusive learning environment [citation:proposal_001:chunk_3]. This approach aligns with the National Education Policy (NEP) 2020's emphasis on holistic development and values-based education.

## Sustainability

Diksha Foundation's KHEL program is designed for long-term sustainability, ensuring that the benefits extend far beyond the grant period and continue to empower the 476 students across our 3 KHEL centers in Sarairanjan, Patna, and Bihta. This sustainability is built on five key mechanisms: youth leadership, existing infrastructure, community ownership, diversified funding, and government alignment.

**1. Youth/Community-Led Sustainability:** We are cultivating a pipeline of young leaders who will progressively take ownership of KHEL program delivery. Each year, we will identify and train 9 Young Leaders from within the KHEL student body to serve as peer mediators and sports coaches [citation:proposal_001:chunk_1]. These Young Leaders will receive intensive training in facilitation, conflict resolution, and sports coaching techniques, mentored by our Fellow Teachers [citation:proposal_001:chunk_1]. Our goal is to retain at least 6 of these Young Leaders each year, providing them with stipends and ongoing professional development opportunities to incentivize their continued involvement [citation:proposal_001:chunk_1]. By year 3, we aim for Young Leaders to independently lead at least 50% of the sports and life skills sessions, ensuring program continuity and fostering a sense of ownership within the community [citation:proposal_001:chunk_1].

**2. Existing Infrastructure:** KHEL leverages existing community resources to minimize costs and maximize impact. Our 3 KHEL centers in Patna, Bihta, and Sarairanjan are established spaces within the community, eliminating the need for new construction [citation:proposal_001:chunk_2]. The sports equipment used in KHEL is durable, reusable, and low-cost, ensuring that the program can continue without significant financial investment [citation:proposal_001:chunk_2]. Furthermore, we utilize existing community playing spaces, such as local parks and school grounds, to conduct sports activities, further reducing infrastructure costs [citation:proposal_001:chunk2].

**3. Community Ownership:** We actively engage families and communities to foster a sense of ownership and ensure the long-term success of KHEL. We organize quarterly community open days at each KHEL center, inviting parents and community members to participate in sports activities, life skills workshops, and cultural events [citation:proposal_001:chunk3]. These events not only build community support but also demonstrate the value of integrating sports and education, addressing the common "study vs. play" cultural barrier [citation:proposal_001:chunk3]. We also establish parent committees at each center to provide feedback on program design and implementation, ensuring that KHEL meets the needs and aspirations of the community [citation:proposal_001:chunk3].

**4. Diversified Funding Base:** Diksha Foundation has a proven track record of securing funding from diverse sources. We currently receive support from organizations such as the Azim Premji Foundation and Feeding India [citation:proposal_001:chunk4]. To ensure the long-term financial sustainability of KHEL, we will actively pursue CSR partnerships with companies aligned with our mission of promoting youth development and education, leveraging our CSR-1 registration [citation:proposal_001:chunk4]. We will also explore grant opportunities from foundations and international organizations that support sports-based development programs [citation:proposal_001:chunk4].

**5. Government Alignment:** We align KHEL with relevant government schemes and policies to leverage public resources and ensure program sustainability. We will explore opportunities to integrate KHEL into the Bihar Khel Niti and Mukhyamantri Khel Vikas Yojana, which promote sports development at the grassroots level [citation:proposal_001:chunk5]. By aligning with these initiatives, we can access government funding, technical assistance, and infrastructure support, further strengthening the sustainability of KHEL [citation:proposal_001:chunk5].
