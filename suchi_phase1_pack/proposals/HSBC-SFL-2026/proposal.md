## Problem Statement

Bihar's youth face significant educational and socio-economic challenges, hindering their ability to thrive and contribute to the state's progress. Our Skills for Life program will address these gaps by providing 476 students across our 3 KHEL centers in Sarairanjan, Patna, and Bihta with crucial academic support, digital literacy, sports, social-emotional learning, and civic engagement opportunities.

Despite progress in enrollment, Bihar lags in key educational outcomes. While enrollment in elementary schools has increased, dropout rates remain a concern, particularly among marginalized communities [citation:proposal_001:chunk_1]. Furthermore, learning levels are significantly below national averages, with a large percentage of students unable to perform basic reading and math tasks appropriate for their grade level [citation:proposal_001:chunk_1]. This disparity is further exacerbated by limited access to quality resources and trained educators, especially in rural areas like Sarairanjan and Bihta where our KHEL centers are located. These challenges directly impede the goals of the National Education Policy (NEP) 2020, which emphasizes foundational literacy and numeracy (FLN) and holistic development.

Existing interventions often fall short in addressing the multifaceted needs of adolescents and youth. While academic support is available, it often lacks integration with crucial life skills and social-emotional learning (SEL) components [citation:proposal_001:chunk_2]. Digital literacy, essential for navigating the modern world and accessing employment opportunities, remains limited, particularly for girls [citation:proposal_001:chunk2]. Moreover, opportunities for sports and civic engagement, vital for developing teamwork, leadership, and a sense of social responsibility, are often scarce [citation:proposal_001:chunk_2]. This gap hinders the development of well-rounded individuals equipped to succeed in both their personal and professional lives.

Diksha Foundation's Skills for Life program, targeting 476 students, directly addresses these critical gaps. By providing comprehensive academic support, digital literacy training, sports activities, social-emotional learning (SEE Learning), and civic engagement opportunities at our KHEL centers in Sarairanjan, Patna, and Bihta, we aim to empower youth with the skills and knowledge necessary to overcome these challenges and achieve their full potential. Our integrated approach aligns with HSBC India's Skills for Life focus and the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), particularly SDG 4 (Quality Education) and SDG 5 (Gender Equality), by fostering holistic development and creating pathways to employability and empowerment for young people in Bihar.

### Implementation Plan

The Skills for Life program will empower 476 students across our 3 KHEL centers — Sarairanjan, Patna, and Bihta — through a comprehensive curriculum encompassing academic support, digital literacy, sports, social-emotional learning, and civic engagement. This holistic approach, aligned with HSBC India's Skills for Life focus, will equip young learners with essential skills for future success [citation:proposal_004:chunk_1].

Our program integrates diverse activities into a structured weekly schedule. Academic support in literacy and numeracy forms a core component, delivered through supplementary education aligned with the ASER framework, utilizing workbooks and teaching-learning materials [citation:proposal_004:chunk_1]. Digital literacy sessions will be conducted 3 times per week, leveraging Khan Academy and internal digital literacy modules, with a current device ratio of approximately 1:3 (to be confirmed with latest inventory) [citation:proposal_004:chunk_1]. Sports activities, promoting physical well-being and teamwork, will be held 3 times per week, coached by Fellow Teachers and supported by an External Sports Trainer, utilizing equipment such as balls, mats, and basic sports kits, ensuring an inclusive approach for all genders [citation:proposal_004:chunk_1]. Social-emotional learning (SEL) sessions, delivered by trained Fellow Teachers using an age-appropriate curriculum, will take place once per week, fostering self-awareness, empathy, and responsible decision-making [citation:proposal_004:chunk_1]. We will also organize quarterly community open days and monthly parent-teacher meetings (dates to be finalized) to foster community engagement and support [citation:proposal_004:chunk_1].

Our team of dedicated staff will ensure effective program implementation. Each center will be staffed by a Center Coordinator and Fellow Teachers, with 8 Fellow Teachers across the three centers [citation:proposal_004:chunk_1]. A part-time Sports Coach will work with the Fellow Teachers to deliver sports activities [citation:proposal_004:chunk_1]. The program will be overseen by a Project Lead, ensuring quality and alignment with program goals [citation:proposal_004:chunk_1].

## Team and Capacity

Diksha Foundation's dedicated team will ensure the Skills for Life program delivers impactful skills development to 476 students across Bihar. Our team's structure, qualifications, and experience are designed to provide comprehensive support across our 3 KHEL centers: Sarairanjan, Patna, and Bihta.

The Skills for Life program will be led by a dedicated Project Lead, who will oversee all aspects of implementation, monitoring, and reporting. The Project Lead will have 7+ years of experience in managing educational programs, a Master's degree in Education or a related field, and a proven track record of achieving program goals [citation:proposal_001:chunk_1]. Reporting to the Project Lead are 3 Center Coordinators, one for each KHEL center (Sarairanjan, Patna, and Bihta). Each Center Coordinator will have 5+ years of experience in community development and educational program management, holding a Bachelor's degree in Social Work or a related field [citation:proposal_001:chunk_1]. They will be responsible for the day-to-day operations of their respective centers, including student enrollment, staff supervision, and community engagement.

Our Fellow Teachers are the backbone of our academic and social-emotional learning initiatives. We will have 8 Fellow Teachers in total, deployed as follows: KHEL Patna: 3 Fellow Teachers, KHEL Bihta: 3 Fellow Teachers, and KHEL Sarairanjan: 2 Fellow Teachers. All Fellow Teachers hold at minimum a Bachelor's degree in Education and have 2+ years of experience in teaching and mentoring children from marginalized communities [citation:proposal_001:chunk_1]. Furthermore, all Fellow Teachers will receive specialized training in SEE Learning to effectively deliver social-emotional learning modules [citation:proposal_001:chunk_1]. A part-time Sports Coach will provide sports training across all three centers, with 3+ years of experience in coaching various sports and promoting physical well-being among children [citation:proposal_001:chunk_1]. An IT volunteer will support digital literacy initiatives and maintain the computer infrastructure at each center [citation:proposal_001:chunk_1].

To ensure program quality and impact, an M&E Officer will be responsible for data collection, analysis, and reporting. The M&E Officer will have 3+ years of experience in monitoring and evaluation of social programs, holding a Master's degree in Statistics or a related field [citation:proposal_001:chunk_1]. This structure ensures effective program delivery and continuous improvement, aligning with HSBC India's Skills for Life objectives.

## Monitoring and Evaluation

Our robust monitoring and evaluation (M&E) framework will ensure the Skills for Life program achieves its goals of empowering 476 students across our 3 KHEL centers in Sarairanjan, Patna, and Bihta. We will track progress against key indicators related to academic support, digital literacy, sports participation, social-emotional learning, and civic engagement, allowing for data-driven course correction throughout the project.

Our M&E system incorporates multiple data collection methods to provide a comprehensive picture of program impact. Fellow Teachers will maintain daily attendance registers and administer ASER-style literacy and numeracy assessments, as well as digital literacy practical tests [citation:proposal_001:chunk_3]. Sports participation will be tracked via logs maintained by the External Sports Trainer and part-time Sports Coach [citation:proposal_001:chunk_3]. Social-emotional learning will be monitored using SEL observation checklists [citation:proposal_001:chunk_3]. Center Coordinators will consolidate data into fortnightly progress reports [citation:proposal_001:chunk_3]. Our M&E Officer will oversee the entire process, ensuring data quality through monthly verification checks [citation:proposal_001:chunk_3]. The Project Lead will conduct quarterly spot-checks to further validate the data [citation:proposal_001:chunk_3].

Data will be collected at three key points: a baseline in Month 1, a midline in Month 6, and an endline in Month 12 [citation:proposal_001:chunk_3]. This allows us to measure growth and adapt our strategies as needed. Data from the KHEL centers will be compiled into an internal digital dashboard, updated fortnightly, providing real-time insights into program performance [citation:proposal_001:chunk_3]. We will provide monthly consolidated progress reports, quarterly narrative and financial reports, and an annual impact report to HSBC India [citation:proposal_001:chunk_3].

### Sustainability

The Skills for Life program will ensure lasting impact for 476 students across our 3 KHEL centers (Sarairanjan, Patna, and Bihta) through youth leadership pipelines, leveraging existing infrastructure, fostering community ownership, and maintaining a diversified funding base.

Our youth leadership pipeline will ensure program continuity by training students as future facilitators. We will select 9 Young Leaders from among our KHEL participants to undergo intensive training in sports coaching, digital literacy, and social-emotional learning [citation:proposal_003:chunk_11]. These Young Leaders will initially co-facilitate sessions with Fellow Teachers, progressively taking on more responsibility [citation:proposal_003:chunk_11]. Our goal is to have at least 7 of these Young Leaders independently leading sessions by the end of the grant period, creating a sustainable model for peer-to-peer learning and mentorship [citation:proposal_003:chunk_11]. This approach not only ensures program continuation but also empowers youth with valuable leadership and facilitation skills, aligning with HSBC India's focus on youth empowerment [citation:proposal_003:chunk_11].

The Skills for Life program will leverage Diksha Foundation's existing infrastructure to minimize costs and maximize efficiency. Our 3 KHEL centers in Patna, Bihta, and Sarairanjan provide dedicated spaces for learning and activities [citation:proposal_003:chunk_11]. We also utilize community playing fields and sports equipment, ensuring no new facilities require construction and equipment is reusable and low-cost [citation:proposal_003:chunk_11]. This approach allows us to focus resources on program delivery and capacity building rather than capital expenditures.

Community ownership is crucial for the long-term success of the Skills for Life program. We actively engage parents and community members through regular meetings and events at our KHEL centers [citation:proposal_003:chunk_11]. These gatherings showcase student progress, promote the importance of education and sports, and address cultural barriers such as the perceived conflict between "study vs play" [citation:proposal_003:chunk_11]. By demonstrating the positive impact of the program on their children's academic performance, social-emotional development, and overall well-being, we foster a sense of ownership and encourage continued support from families and the wider community [citation:proposal_003:chunk_11].

Diksha Foundation maintains a diversified funding base to ensure long-term financial stability. In addition to HSBC India (via Swades Foundation), we receive support from organizations such as the Azim Premji Foundation and Feeding India [citation:proposal_003:chunk_11]. We will actively seek additional funding opportunities from other foundations, corporations, and government agencies to support the continued operation and expansion of the Skills for Life program beyond the grant period [citation:proposal_003:chunk_11]. This diversified approach reduces our reliance on any single funding source and ensures the sustainability of our programs.
