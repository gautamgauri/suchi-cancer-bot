## Problem Statement

Bihar faces significant challenges in foundational literacy and numeracy (FLN), hindering the potential of its young population. Rusty Tutor Bot will address this critical gap by providing personalized, AI-driven learning support to 476 students across our 3 KHEL centers in Sarairanjan, Patna, and Bihta.

Despite progress in enrollment, learning outcomes in Bihar remain below national averages. ASER 2022 data indicates that a significant percentage of children in early grades struggle with basic reading and arithmetic skills [citation:proposal_001:chunk_1]. This is further compounded by high dropout rates, particularly among marginalized communities and adolescent girls [citation:proposal_001:chunk_1]. Existing educational interventions often lack the resources to provide individualized attention, leaving many students behind [citation:proposal_001:chunk_1]. Our KHEL centers in Patna, Bihta, and Sarairanjan serve communities where these challenges are acutely felt [citation:proposal_001:chunk_1]. The National Education Policy (NEP) 2020 emphasizes the importance of foundational learning and the use of technology to enhance educational outcomes [citation:proposal_001:chunk_1]. However, effective implementation requires innovative solutions that can bridge the resource gap and cater to diverse learning needs.

The current educational landscape in Bihar lacks personalized learning approaches at scale, especially those leveraging AI to adapt to individual student needs [citation:proposal_001:chunk_1]. While digital literacy programs exist, they often do not provide the targeted support necessary to address specific learning gaps in literacy and numeracy [citation:proposal_001:chunk_1]. Furthermore, access to quality educational resources and trained teachers is unevenly distributed across the state, with rural areas like Sarairanjan facing greater challenges [citation:proposal_001:chunk_1]. Rusty Tutor Bot directly addresses this gap by providing personalized learning experiences through an AI-powered tutor, accessible via WhatsApp, ensuring that each of the 476 students in our KHEL Sarairanjan, KHEL Patna, and KHEL Bihta centers receive the support they need to succeed. By focusing on foundational literacy and numeracy, and integrating Social and Emotional Learning (SEL) principles, we aim to improve learning outcomes and empower students to reach their full potential, aligning with the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) related to quality education [citation:proposal_001:chunk_1].

### Scale-up Plan

The Rusty Tutor Bot program will be sustained through community ownership, leveraging existing infrastructure, and diversified funding, ensuring continued impact for 476 students across our 3 KHEL centers [citation:proposal_001:chunk_1]. Our approach emphasizes youth leadership, integrating the program into existing community structures, and building a diversified funding base to ensure long-term viability.

**1. Youth/Community-Led Sustainability:** We are committed to developing local leadership to sustain the Rusty Tutor Bot program beyond the grant period. We will train 9 Young Leaders across our 3 KHEL centers (3 per center) to serve as mentors and facilitators [citation:proposal_001:chunk_2]. These Young Leaders will undergo a structured training program covering AI fluency, tutoring methodologies, and SEL techniques, including SEE Learning principles [citation:proposal_001:chunk_3]. The training will be delivered by our experienced Fellow Teachers and Center Coordinators, with ongoing support from the Project Lead. Our goal is to retain at least 75% of these Young Leaders in active roles for at least one year post-grant, creating a pipeline of skilled individuals who can independently manage and expand the program [citation:proposal_001:chunk_4].

**2. Existing Infrastructure:** The Rusty Tutor Bot program will leverage Diksha Foundation's established infrastructure to minimize costs and ensure sustainability. Our 3 KHEL centers in Patna, Bihta, and Sarairanjan provide existing learning spaces equipped with computers and internet access [citation:proposal_001:chunk_1]. These centers already serve 476 learners, creating a ready-made platform for integrating the AI tutor [citation:proposal_001:chunk_1]. Furthermore, our partnership with local schools through the Empowering Futures program provides access to a broader network of students and resources. No new facilities require construction, and equipment is reusable and low-cost, further reducing the financial burden [citation:proposal_001:chunk5].

**3. Community Ownership:** We foster community ownership through active parent engagement and community events. We will organize quarterly community open days at each KHEL center to showcase student progress and demonstrate the benefits of the Rusty Tutor Bot program [citation:proposal_001:chunk_6]. These events will also serve as a platform for gathering feedback and incorporating community input into the program design. We will also conduct regular parent-teacher meetings to discuss student performance and provide guidance on how parents can support their children's learning at home. By linking education with sports, we address the "study vs. play" cultural barrier, demonstrating how both contribute to holistic development [citation:proposal_001:chunk_7].

**4. Diversified Funding Base:** Diksha Foundation has a proven track record of securing funding from diverse sources. We currently receive support from organizations such as the Azim Premji Foundation and Feeding India [citation:proposal_001:chunk_8]. To ensure the long-term sustainability of the Rusty Tutor Bot program, we will actively pursue additional funding opportunities, including CSR partnerships with local businesses and grants from philanthropic organizations. We will also explore opportunities to integrate the program into government initiatives, such as the Bihar Education Project Council, aligning with NEP 2020 goals [citation:proposal_001:chunk_9].
