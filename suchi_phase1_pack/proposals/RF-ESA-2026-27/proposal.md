### Background and Problem Statement and Proposed Solution

Gully Goal will empower 476 students across Bihar by bridging the gap in access to quality education and sports, particularly for marginalized communities, through a comprehensive football-for-development program [citation:proposal_001:chunk_1]. Diksha Foundation proposes to address critical educational disparities in Bihar, where, despite progress, significant challenges persist in enrollment, retention, and learning outcomes [citation:proposal_001:chunk_2]. While enrollment rates have improved, dropout rates remain a concern, especially among girls and children from marginalized communities, hindering their access to quality education and opportunities for holistic development [citation:proposal_001:chunk_3].

Bihar faces unique challenges, including socioeconomic disparities, infrastructure limitations, and gender inequalities, which exacerbate educational gaps [citation:proposal_001:chunk_4]. Existing interventions often fall short of addressing the holistic needs of children, neglecting the crucial role of sports and social-emotional learning in fostering overall well-being and academic success [citation:proposal_001:chunk_5]. Our KHEL (Knowledge Hub for Education and Learning) program, operating in Sarairanjan, Patna, and Bihta, directly tackles these challenges by integrating digital literacy, sports coaching, and social-emotional learning into a comprehensive educational experience [citation:proposal_001:chunk_6]. This integrated approach aligns with the National Education Policy (NEP) 2020's emphasis on holistic development and aims to equip children with the skills and knowledge necessary to thrive in the 21st century [citation:proposal_001:chunk_7].

Gully Goal directly responds to these challenges by providing structured football-for-development programming that enhances physical health, promotes teamwork, and builds leadership skills [citation:proposal_001:chunk_8]. By combining sports with digital literacy and social-emotional learning, we create a supportive and engaging learning environment that fosters academic achievement, personal growth, and social responsibility among 476 students [citation:proposal_001:chunk_9]. This initiative directly contributes to Sustainable Development Goal (SDG) 4, ensuring inclusive and equitable quality education and promoting lifelong learning opportunities for all [citation:proposal_001:chunk_10].

### Objectives of the Project

Gully Goal will empower 476 students across our 3 KHEL centers in Bihar with improved learning outcomes, enhanced digital literacy, and crucial life skills, fostering a generation of confident and capable young leaders. This project directly aligns with Reliance Foundation's focus on integrating digital access into education, empowering girls, and strengthening grassroots sports [citation:proposal_001:chunk_3]. Our objectives are designed to be Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Time-bound (SMART), with clear indicators to track progress over the 12-month grant period.

1.  **Improved Learning Outcomes:** We aim to improve grade-level reading comprehension among KHEL students by at least 20% by Month 12, as measured by ASER-style reading assessments [citation:proposal_001:chunk_3]. Baseline performance will be established in Month 1, and progress will be tracked quarterly. This objective directly addresses the need for improved learning outcomes in Bihar, aligning with NEP 2020 goals [citation:proposal_001:chunk_3].

2.  **Increased Attendance and Retention:** We will increase average daily attendance across the 3 KHEL centers (Sarairanjan, Patna, and Bihta) by 15% by Month 6 and sustain this level through Month 12 [citation:proposal_001:chunk_3]. Attendance will be tracked daily via attendance registers, with baseline attendance established in Month 1. We will also maintain a student retention rate of at least 90% throughout the project period, addressing drop-out through targeted counseling and community engagement [citation:proposal_001:chunk_3].

3.  **Enhanced Sports and Physical Activity:** We will ensure that at least 80% of students actively participate in the sports coaching (football) sessions at least once a week, as recorded through session attendance logs [citation:proposal_001:chunk_3]. This will be measured through weekly attendance records maintained by the Sports Coach, starting in Month 1. This objective promotes physical well-being and teamwork, aligning with Reliance Foundation's focus on grassroots sports development [citation:proposal_001:chunk_3].

4.  **Improved Digital Skills:** We aim to increase the percentage of students demonstrating proficiency in basic digital literacy skills (e.g., using educational software, internet navigation) by 30% by Month 12 [citation:proposal_001:chunk_3]. Proficiency will be assessed through practical skills tests administered by the Computer Instructor, with a baseline established in Month 1. Digital literacy training will be delivered through 3 sessions per week.

5.  **Enhanced Life Skills and Agency:** We will improve students' self-reported confidence and problem-solving abilities by 25% by Month 12, as measured by pre- and post-project surveys incorporating validated scales for self-esteem and agency [citation:proposal_001:chunk_3]. These surveys will be administered by Fellow Teachers trained in SEE Learning. This objective fosters leadership development and empowers girls, aligning with Reliance Foundation's key themes [citation:proposal_001:chunk_3].

6.  **Strengthened Teacher Capacity:** We will train all 8 Fellow Teachers in Social-Emotional Learning (SEE Learning) methodologies by Month 3, as evidenced by certification of completion [citation:proposal_001:chunk_3]. Furthermore, we will conduct quarterly refresher workshops to ensure consistent implementation of SEE Learning principles in the classroom. This objective ensures the sustainability of the project's impact by equipping teachers with the skills to foster holistic development.

### Sustainability

Gully Goal will ensure lasting impact by cultivating youth leadership, leveraging existing infrastructure, and fostering community ownership to sustain the program's benefits beyond the grant period. We are committed to embedding the program within the existing community fabric, ensuring its continuation and expansion.

**Youth/Community-Led Sustainability:** A core element of our sustainability strategy involves training and empowering young leaders from the communities we serve. We will identify and train 9 Young Leaders across the 3 KHEL centers (Sarairanjan, Patna, and Bihta) as football coaches and mentors [citation:proposal_001:chunk_3]. These Young Leaders will undergo intensive training in Football3 methodology, sports coaching, and leadership development, equipping them to progressively lead sessions independently. Our goal is to retain at least 6 of these Young Leaders as volunteer coaches after the grant period, ensuring the continuation of sports activities and mentorship for the 476 students [citation:proposal_001:chunk_3]. This approach not only provides sustainable coaching capacity but also fosters youth empowerment and leadership within the community.

**Existing Infrastructure:** Diksha Foundation leverages existing infrastructure to minimize costs and ensure long-term viability. Our 3 KHEL centers in Patna, Bihta, and Sarairanjan, already serving 476 learners, provide a stable base for program activities [citation:proposal_001:chunk_3]. Furthermore, we utilize existing community playing spaces for football training, eliminating the need for new construction. The equipment used in the program is reusable and low-cost, further reducing the financial burden of sustaining the program.

**Community Ownership:** We actively engage families and communities to foster a sense of ownership and responsibility for the program's success. We will also establish parent committees at each center to provide feedback, support program implementation, and advocate for the program's continuation. By involving families and communities in the program's design and implementation, we ensure its relevance and sustainability.
