## Red flags (seek urgent care now)

If you experience any of these symptoms, seek immediate medical attention:

- **Severe shortness of breath or chest pain** - Can indicate serious complications
- **Fainting or confusion** - May signal neurological issues or severe infection
- **Uncontrolled bleeding** - Any bleeding that doesn't stop with pressure
- **Vomiting blood** - Requires immediate emergency care
- **High fever with severe weakness** - Especially dangerous during chemotherapy when immunity is low (febrile neutropenia is a medical emergency)
- **Severe abdominal pain** - Can indicate various serious conditions
- **Signs of infection** - High fever (above 100.4°F / 38°C), chills, rapid heartbeat (especially during treatment)
- **Sudden severe headache with vision changes** - May indicate neurological emergency
- **Inability to eat or drink for more than 24 hours** - Risk of dehydration
- **Swelling, redness, or warmth at surgical site** - May indicate infection

## What to do now

1. **Call for help immediately:**
   - **112** (national emergency number)
   - **108** (ambulance service - available across India)
   - **102** (medical emergency helpline, available in many states)
2. **Go to the nearest emergency department** - Don't wait if symptoms are severe
3. **Contact your oncology team** - If you're in treatment, notify them immediately
4. **Bring someone with you** - Have a family member or friend accompany you if possible

## What to bring to the emergency department

- Government ID (Aadhaar card)
- Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY) card if available
- Current medication list
- Recent medical reports and discharge summaries
- Contact details of your treating oncologist

## Important notes

- Trust your instincts - if something feels wrong, seek help
- It's better to be cautious than to wait
- Keep emergency contact numbers easily accessible
- Have your medical history and current medications list ready
- If you are on chemotherapy, fever is always an emergency - do not wait
