Based on comprehensive research into the healthcare landscape of Bihar, the following report details the cancer treatment centers that hold National Accreditation Board for Hospitals & Healthcare Providers (NABH) accreditation or recommendation. The report focuses on departmental structures, infrastructure, and key medical specialists.

## Executive Summary: Cancer Care and Accreditation in Bihar

The landscape of oncology in Bihar has shifted significantly with the entry of major multi-specialty hospital chains and the upgrading of local institutions. NABH accreditation serves as a critical benchmark for patient safety and quality of care. In Patna, the capital city, several private tertiary care hospitals have achieved full NABH accreditation and established dedicated cancer institutes or departments offering comprehensive modalities: Medical, Surgical, and Radiation Oncology.

While apex government institutes like AIIMS Patna and IGIMS offer high-level cancer care, they do not currently hold full NABH accreditation according to the latest registries. Therefore, this report prioritizes private sector hospitals that explicitly meet the criteria for NABH status, followed by specialized cancer centers that have received NABH recommendations for CGHS empanelment.

## 1. Premier NABH Accredited Multi-Specialty Hospitals with Oncology

These hospitals hold full NABH accreditation and feature integrated cancer centers with advanced infrastructure (PET-CT, Linear Accelerators, Robotic Surgery).

### Paras HMRI Hospital, Patna

Paras HMRI was the first hospital in Bihar to receive NABH accreditation. It houses the Paras Cancer Centre, a comprehensive facility offering multidisciplinary cancer care.

- **Accreditation Status:** NABH Accredited
- **Location:** Raja Bazar, Bailey Road, Patna

#### Departments and Infrastructure
The cancer center is equipped with a Linear Accelerator (LINAC), PET-CT, and Gamma Camera. It offers:
- **Medical Oncology:** Chemotherapy, Targeted Therapy, Immunotherapy, and Hormone Therapy.
- **Radiation Oncology:** IMRT, IGRT, VMAT, and Brachytherapy.
- **Surgical Oncology:** Organ-preserving surgeries and complex tumor resections.
- **Hemat-Oncology:** Bone Marrow Transplants (BMT) for leukemia and lymphoma.

#### Key Doctors
- **Dr. Abhishek Anand** (Director & HOD - Medical Oncology): Specializes in solid tumors, lymphomas, and stem cell transplants. DM in Medical Oncology.
- **Dr. Shekhar Kumar Keshri** (Director & HOD - Radiation Oncology): Over 21 years of experience, specializes in Head & Neck, Breast, and Cervical cancers (SBRT and SRS).
- **Dr. Mitali Dandekar Lall** (Chief Consultant & HOD - Surgical Oncology): Expert in Head and Neck oncology with over 21 years of experience.
- **Dr. R. N. Tagore** (Chief Consultant - Medical Oncology): 31 years of experience in targeted and palliative therapies.
- **Dr. Mosarrat Shaheen** (Consultant - Medical Oncology): Specializes in Precision Oncology and immunotherapy.

### Jay Prabha Medanta Super Specialty Hospital, Patna

Part of the renowned Medanta group, this facility is the largest private hospital in Patna and holds both NABH and NABL accreditation. It operates a dedicated Cancer Institute.

- **Accreditation Status:** NABH and NABL Accredited
- **Location:** Kankarbagh, Patna

#### Departments and Infrastructure
The hospital utilizes the Varian Edge radiation machine (a first for Eastern India) and offers Da Vinci Robotic Surgery for precise oncological procedures.
- **Departments:** Medical Oncology, Radiation Oncology, Surgical Oncology, Haemato-Oncology, and Breast Services.
- **Specialized Clinics:** Gynae-Oncology, Uro-Oncology, and Head & Neck Oncology.

#### Key Doctors
- **Dr. Ravi Shankar Singh** (Medical Director & Senior Medical Oncologist): Expertise in solid tumor malignancies.
- **Dr. Rajiv Ranjan Prasad** (Director - Radiation Oncology): 30+ years of experience; expert in IMRT, IGRT, and SRS/SRT.
- **Dr. Sundeep Kumar** (Associate Director - Surgical Oncology): Specializes in GI, Gynae, and Head & Neck oncology, with robotic surgery expertise.
- **Dr. Amarendra Amar** (Associate Director - Medical Oncology): Focuses on prostate and breast cancer, immunotherapy, and thoracic oncology.
- **Dr. Amit Kumar** (Associate Director - Medical Oncology): Specializes in adult chemotherapy and bone marrow transplantation.
- **Dr. Niharika Roy** (Senior Consultant - Breast Services): Specializes in breast conservation and oncoplastic surgery.

### Ruban Memorial Hospital, Patna

Ruban Memorial is a 450-bed multi-specialty quaternary care center with full NABH accreditation. It is a leader in robotic oncology surgery.

- **Accreditation Status:** Full NABH Accreditation
- **Location:** Patliputra Colony, Patna

#### Departments and Infrastructure
The hospital features a Department of Oncology & Onco Surgery and serves as a high-volume center for urological and gastrointestinal cancers.
- **Robotic Surgery:** For Prostate, Kidney, Colorectal, and Gynecologic cancers.
- **Core Services:** Chemotherapy, Surgical Oncology, and Hematological Malignancies.

#### Key Doctors
- **Dr. Sanjeev Kumar** (Consultant Onco Surgeon): 8+ years of experience in surgical oncology.
- **Dr. Arun Pandey** (Director & HOD - Surgical Oncology & Robotic Surgery): Leads the robotic surgery unit.
- **Dr. Satyajit Kumar Singh** (Managing Director & Urologist): Senior specialist handling Uro-oncology cases.
- **Dr. Ravi Ranjan Kumar** (Consultant - Robotic & Thoracic Surgery): Performed Bihar's first VATS decortication.

## 2. Specialized Cancer Centers with NABH Recommendation

Several hospitals dedicated specifically to cancer care are listed as "Recommended" by NABH for CGHS (Central Government Health Scheme) empanelment.

### Buddha Cancer Centre, Patna

A dedicated cancer hospital established to provide comprehensive care.

- **Accreditation Status:** NABH Recommended (Recommendation Date: 17 Nov 2023)
- **Location:** Bailey Road, Patna

#### Departments
- **Medical Oncology:** Targeted therapy, genetic testing (NGS), and immunotherapy.
- **Surgical Oncology:** Organ-specific surgeries (Head & Neck, Breast, Gynae).
- **Hemato-Oncology:** Treatment for leukemia, lymphoma, and myeloma.
- **Pain & Palliative Care:** Dedicated clinic for symptom management.

#### Key Doctors
- **Dr. Arvind Kumar** (Founder Director & Senior Medical Oncologist): First doctor to pursue DM in Medical Oncology in Bihar (AIIMS Delhi alumnus).
- **Dr. Rahul Kumar Chaudhary** (Consultant - Surgical Oncology): Authority on gallbladder cancer with fellowship from Japan.
- **Dr. Rayaz Ahmed** (Senior Hematologist): DM from CMC Vellore; expert in bone marrow transplants.
- **Dr. Tanveer Abdul Majeed** (Surgical Oncologist): Specializes in GI and Thoracic oncology.

---

### Mahavir Cancer Sansthan and Research Centre, Patna

One of the largest charitable cancer hospitals in India, serving patients across Bihar, Jharkhand, and Nepal.

- **Accreditation Status:** NABH Recommended (Recommendation Date: 02 Jan 2024)
- **Location:** Phulwari Sharif, Patna

#### Departments
- **Radiation Oncology:** Linear Accelerators, HDR Brachytherapy, IMRT, and IGRT.
- **Medical Oncology:** 30-bed dedicated unit for chemotherapy and immunotherapy.
- **Surgical Oncology:** Advanced operation theaters for complex resections.
- **Nuclear Medicine & Preventive Oncology.**

#### Key Doctors
- **Dr. (Prof) Biswajit Sanyal** (Director Administration): Veteran radiation oncologist trained in London.
- **Dr. Manisha Singh** (Medical Director & HOD - Medical Oncology): DNB and ESMO certified.
- **Dr. Vinita Trivedi** (HOD - Radiation Oncology): Leads the radiotherapy department.
- **Dr. (Prof.) Chiranjiva Khandelwal** (HOD - Surgical Oncology): FRCS qualified surgeon.

---

### S.S. Hospital & Research Centre, Patna

A multi-specialty center with strong oncology focus, led by one of the state's most prominent oncologists.

- **Accreditation Status:** NABH Recommended (Recommendation Date: 16 Apr 2024)
- **Location:** Kankarbagh, Patna

#### Departments
- **Medical Oncology:** Day-care center with Chemo Admix Unit (zero drug wastage).
- **Surgical Oncology:** Organ preservation and minimally invasive surgery.

#### Key Doctors
- **Dr. J.K. Singh** (Director & Senior Oncologist): Padma Shri awardee with over 49 years of experience.
- **Dr. Tanveer Majid** (Surgical Oncologist): 29+ years of experience in cancer surgery.
- **Dr. Sweta Das** (Medical & Clinical Oncology): Also serves as Clinical Geneticist.
