## Mahavir Cancer Sansthan, Patna

Mahavir Cancer Sansthan (MCSRC) is a 618-bed comprehensive cancer hospital in Phulwarisharif, Patna run by the Mahavir Mandir Trust. It is Bihar's only super-specialty cancer centre. Departments include surgical oncology, medical oncology and radiation oncology, as well as radiology and nuclear medicine. The hospital focuses on affordable care: pediatric patients are treated free and low-income adults receive financial assistance. Contact: Phulwarisharif, Patna-801505; Phone: +91-612-2250127/2253956/3222583.

### How to Visit
1. Plan your visit by checking the hospital OPD schedule (arrive in morning).
2. At the hospital, register at the OPD desk with a photo ID and any referral slip.
3. Attend the appropriate OPD (e.g. Medical or Radiation Oncology) based on doctor's referral.
4. After the doctor's consultation, complete any prescribed tests (laboratory, imaging) within the hospital.
5. Review test results with the doctor and follow-up as advised (admission or further OPDs).
6. Keep an organized file of all test reports, prescriptions, and appointment slips for each visit.

## AIIMS Patna (Phulwarisharif) Cancer Centre

All India Institute of Medical Sciences (AIIMS) Patna (Phulwarisharif) is a government tertiary-care hospital with a developing oncology department. Its Radiation Oncology dept. has a modern linear accelerator and brachytherapy unit. The department runs specialized clinics (head and neck, breast, pediatric oncology) and multidisciplinary tumor boards. AIIMS Patna provides free cancer screening camps and aims to deliver advanced care at low cost. Contact: Phulwarisharif, Patna-801507; Tel +91-612-2451070.

### How to Visit
1. Register for an OPD appointment through the AIIMS ORS portal (ors.gov.in) or at the hospital reception.
2. On the appointment day, arrive at the OPD block (Basement, LGB block) before the scheduled time.
3. Carry all referrals, ID (Aadhaar), and insurance/PM-JAY information.
4. Attend the oncology OPD for consultation and obtain any required test orders.
5. Complete prescribed tests (labs, imaging) in the hospital and return for follow-up.
6. Collect medicines from the hospital pharmacy as prescribed.
7. Maintain all medical records (reports, prescriptions) in one file for ongoing care.

## IGIMS (State Cancer Institute), Patna

Indira Gandhi Institute of Medical Sciences (IGIMS), Sheikhpura in Patna is a state government tertiary hospital with a dedicated oncology department. It offers chemotherapy and has radiation therapy facilities. IGIMS provides highly subsidized or free cancer treatment for low-income patients. Contact: Sheikhpura, Patna-800014; Tel: 0612-2297631, 0612-2297099.

### How to Visit
1. Register for the IGIMS OPD (online app/website or at the registration counter).
2. Registration fee is modest (e.g. Rs 50) and OPD runs Monday-Friday 8:30am-4:30pm (Saturday till noon).
3. Bring a government ID (or Aadhaar) and any medical records or referrals.
4. Visit the Oncology OPD on your appointment day and consult the doctor.
5. After consultation, complete any prescribed investigations (labs, scans) in the hospital.
6. Follow up with the doctor to review test results and plan further treatment.

## Ayushman Bharat - Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (PM-JAY)

Ayushman Bharat-PMJAY is a national health insurance scheme providing cashless treatment up to Rs 5 lakh per family per year. It covers major illnesses including cancer. Eligibility is based on socio-economic criteria determined by the state (targeting poor families). Enroll via your PDS/BPL list; beneficiaries receive an e-card. At an empanelled hospital (including Mahavir, IGIMS, AIIMS Patna), present your PM-JAY card or Aadhaar at admission. No upfront payment is required for covered treatment packages. If authorisation is denied or delayed, contact the PM-JAY helpline at 14555.

### How to Use PM-JAY
1. Confirm your eligibility (based on BPL/AAY or state-determined criteria).
2. Obtain a PM-JAY e-card (or prepare to use Aadhaar at the hospital for verification).
3. Go to an empanelled hospital and present your e-card and a photo ID at the Ayushman counter.
4. Request pre-authorization for the required treatment (hospital staff will assist).
5. Ensure all hospital bills and forms are documented under PM-JAY to avoid out-of-pocket payment.
6. For any issues, call the PM-JAY helpline (14555) for assistance.

## Emergency Ambulance Services (108/102) in Bihar

In Bihar, dial 108 for any critical medical emergency (accident, chest pain, serious illness) and 102 specifically for transport of pregnant women or newborns. These toll-free ambulance services are operated by the National Health Mission/JSSK and are free for eligible patients. Clearly provide your location, phone number and patient details when calling. Also remember, 112 is the general emergency helpline for all incidents.

### In an Emergency
1. In a medical crisis, determine if the situation is life-threatening or involves a pregnant/newborn patient.
2. If it is a general emergency (trauma, medical) dial **108**. If the emergency involves a pregnant woman or newborn, dial **102**.
3. When connected, give your exact location, describe the emergency briefly, and provide a contact number.
4. Follow any instructions the dispatcher gives (they may advise on first aid while the ambulance is en route).
5. Stay on the line until the dispatcher confirms help is coming and has all information.

## Caregiver Logistics: Travel, OPD Visits, and Record-Keeping

Plan ahead for hospital visits in Patna. Mahavir Cancer Sansthan is approximately 7 km from Patna Junction railway station and approximately 13 km from the city bus stand (autos available). The nearest airport (Patna) is approximately 5 km away. Mahavir provides an affordable dharmashala (guest lodging) near the hospital. For OPD days, arrive early and carry photo ID, any doctor's referral or appointment slip, and insurance or PM-JAY/ABHA details. Pack essentials: water, snacks, and a file with previous medical reports and prescriptions.

### Planning Your Visit
1. Arrange travel: book train, bus or flight to Patna, then take an auto/taxi to the hospital.
2. Arrange lodging: consider Mahavir's dharmashala (low-cost) or local hotels near the hospital.
3. On the OPD visit day, arrive 30-60 minutes early. Bring ID (Aadhaar), referral/appointment slip, and insurance or PM-JAY/ABHA information.
4. Register at the hospital reception/OPD desk and retain the registration token or slip.
5. After the consultation, complete all prescribed diagnostics (labs, scans) and then return to the doctor with results.
6. Fill any prescriptions at the hospital pharmacy or a trusted local chemist.
7. Maintain a folder of all reports, prescriptions and receipts for future reference.

## Breast Cancer: Diagnosis and Treatment Pathway in India

Any new breast lump or concerning change should be evaluated immediately by a doctor. Diagnosis typically involves a clinical exam followed by imaging (mammogram/ultrasound) and a biopsy. Breast cancer treatment usually combines surgery (lumpectomy or mastectomy), radiation, and medications. Systemic therapies may include chemotherapy, hormone-blockers or targeted drugs depending on tumor markers. Caregivers should ask the medical team key questions: cancer subtype, stage, treatment options, and side effects.

### Steps After Noticing a Symptom
1. Schedule a doctor's visit at the first sign of a breast lump or symptom.
2. Undergo recommended tests: get a mammogram and/or breast ultrasound, and a core needle biopsy for confirmation.
3. Collect the pathology report (note tumor size, grade, ER/PR/HER2 receptor status).
4. Meet the oncologist to discuss a treatment plan (may include surgery, chemo, radiation, hormone/targeted therapy).
5. Prepare questions: ask about type/stage of cancer, treatment timeline, expected side effects, and supportive care.
6. Bring identification and all previous medical records (reports, scans, treatment summaries) to each appointment.

## Financial Aid and Charitable Support for Cancer Patients in India

Numerous aid programs exist for cancer patients. Hospital social welfare officers help patients apply for government schemes like Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY) and Rashtriya Arogya Nidhi, as well as Prime Minister's and Chief Minister's relief funds. PM-JAY covers up to Rs 5 lakh per family for eligible patients. NGOs also assist: the Indian Cancer Society's Cancer Cure Fund offers financial aid to low-income patients nationwide (helpline 1800-22-1951). At Mahavir Cancer Sansthan, Mahavir Mandir Trust provides free treatment for children and a Rs 10,000 grant for poor adult patients.

### How to Access Financial Aid
1. Gather documents: ID, income certificate/BPL card, and medical records to prove eligibility.
2. Visit the hospital's Medical Social Welfare desk to apply for aid schemes (PM-JAY, RAN, PMNRF, CM Relief).
3. Ensure you have an Ayushman Bharat card and get pre-authorisation for treatment if applicable.
4. Call non-profits like the Indian Cancer Society (1800-22-1951) for guidance on their financial assistance programs.
5. At Mahavir Cancer Sansthan, inquire about Mandir Trust assistance (free pediatric care, Rs 10,000 aid for poor adults).
6. Retain all medical bills, prescriptions, and test reports - these are needed when applying for reimbursements or charity grants.
