## Overview

India has a network of government and private cancer treatment centers. Major tertiary cancer centers are located in metropolitan cities, while regional cancer centers serve state-level populations. Government hospitals generally offer subsidized or free treatment for eligible patients.

## Bihar

### Mahavir Cancer Sansthan (MCS), Patna
- **Type:** Dedicated cancer hospital (government-aided)
- **Location:** Phulwarisharif, Patna, Bihar
- **Services:** Surgical oncology, medical oncology (chemotherapy), radiation oncology, pathology, radiology, palliative care
- **Facilities:** Operation theatres, ICU, blood bank, pharmacy, diagnostic imaging
- **Key feature:** One of the largest dedicated cancer hospitals in eastern India
- **Contact:** Available through hospital reception
- **How to access:** Walk-in OPD registration available; referral from district hospital helpful but not mandatory

### IGIMS (Indira Gandhi Institute of Medical Sciences), Patna
- **Type:** Government medical institute with cancer care facilities
- **Location:** Sheikhpura, Patna, Bihar
- **Services:** Oncology department, surgical services, diagnostic services, chemotherapy
- **Key feature:** Multi-specialty government hospital with oncology wing
- **How to access:** OPD registration; referral helpful for faster processing

### State Cancer Institute at IGIMS, Patna
- **Type:** Dedicated cancer wing within IGIMS (under National Programme for Prevention and Control of Cancer, Diabetes, CVD and Stroke - NPCDCS)
- **Services:** Cancer screening, diagnosis, treatment, chemotherapy, follow-up care
- **Key feature:** Established under central government initiative to strengthen state-level cancer care

### AIIMS Patna (All India Institute of Medical Sciences)
- **Type:** Central government premier medical institute
- **Location:** Phulwarisharif, Patna, Bihar
- **Services:** Oncology, surgical oncology, radiation oncology, pathology, radiology
- **Key feature:** Tertiary referral center with multi-disciplinary cancer care
- **How to access:** OPD registration; online appointment booking may be available

### Rajendra Memorial Research Institute (RMRI), Patna
- **Type:** Government research institute with medical services
- **Services:** Limited oncology referral services
- **Note:** Primarily a research institute; cancer patients are often referred to MCS or IGIMS

## When to Seek Care Outside Bihar

Patients may be referred to tertiary centers outside Bihar for:
- Complex surgeries not available locally
- Specialized treatments (bone marrow transplant, certain immunotherapies)
- Clinical trials
- Second opinions for rare cancers

### Common referral destinations from Bihar:
- **Tata Memorial Hospital, Mumbai** - India's premier cancer center (PMJAY empanelled)
- **AIIMS New Delhi** - Comprehensive cancer care
- **Rajiv Gandhi Cancer Institute, Delhi** - Private specialized cancer hospital
- **Homi Bhabha Cancer Hospital, Varanasi** - Tata Memorial Centre affiliate, closer to eastern Bihar
- **Chittaranjan National Cancer Institute, Kolkata** - Major cancer center in eastern India

## Typical Patient Journey in Bihar

### Step 1: Initial Suspicion
- Visit local doctor or district hospital
- Get basic investigations (blood tests, imaging)

### Step 2: Referral to Cancer Center
- Get referral letter from local doctor (helpful but not always mandatory)
- Common first stops: Mahavir Cancer Sansthan or IGIMS Patna

### Step 3: Diagnosis and Staging
- Registration at cancer center OPD
- Biopsy, imaging (CT/MRI/PET scan), blood work
- Pathology report (may take 5-10 working days)
- Staging assessment by oncology team

### Step 4: Treatment Planning
- Multi-disciplinary team (MDT) discussion
- Treatment plan: surgery, chemotherapy, radiation, or combination
- Discussion of costs and financial assistance options

### Step 5: Treatment
- Treatment as per plan
- Regular follow-up visits
- Side effect management

### Step 6: Follow-up
- Regular follow-up as advised (typically every 3-6 months initially)
- Surveillance imaging and blood tests
- Survivorship care

## Documents to Carry for Hospital Visit

Essential documents for any cancer care hospital visit in India:
1. **Government ID** (Aadhaar card, voter ID, or passport)
2. **Ayushman Bharat (PMJAY) card** if available
3. **Previous medical records** (prescriptions, discharge summaries)
4. **Biopsy/pathology reports** (if any)
5. **Imaging CDs/reports** (X-ray, CT, MRI, PET scan)
6. **Blood test reports** (recent, within last 2 weeks)
7. **Referral letter** from local doctor (if available)
8. **Passport-size photographs** (2-3, for hospital registration)
9. **BPL card / income certificate** (for subsidized treatment)
10. **Health insurance documents** (if any private insurance)

## Other States - Major Cancer Centers

### Delhi NCR
- **AIIMS New Delhi** - Premier government medical institute
- **Rajiv Gandhi Cancer Institute and Research Centre** - Private specialized
- **Safdarjung Hospital** - Government hospital with oncology services

### Mumbai / Maharashtra
- **Tata Memorial Hospital** - India's largest cancer center (government)
- **ACTREC (Advanced Centre for Treatment, Research and Education in Cancer)** - Tata Memorial affiliate

### Kolkata / West Bengal
- **Chittaranjan National Cancer Institute** - Major government cancer center
- **Tata Medical Center, Kolkata** - Comprehensive cancer care

### Varanasi / Uttar Pradesh
- **Homi Bhabha Cancer Hospital** - Tata Memorial Centre affiliate
- **BHU (Banaras Hindu University) Hospital** - Government medical institute

### Chennai / Tamil Nadu
- **Cancer Institute (WIA), Adyar** - One of India's oldest cancer centers
- **JIPMER, Puducherry** - Government medical institute

## Important Notes
- Government hospitals generally offer significantly lower costs than private hospitals
- Many government cancer centers have social work departments that help with financial assistance
- PMJAY (Ayushman Bharat) is accepted at most empanelled government and some private hospitals
- Treatment costs vary significantly between government and private facilities
- Waiting times at government hospitals may be longer; plan accordingly
