## Overview

India has a network of government and private cancer treatment centers. Major tertiary cancer centers are located in metropolitan cities, while regional cancer centers serve state-level populations. Government hospitals generally offer subsidized or free treatment for eligible patients.

## Bihar

### Understanding Hospital Accreditation

- **NABH Accredited**: Full accreditation by the National Accreditation Board for Hospitals & Healthcare Providers — indicates hospital meets national quality and patient safety standards
- **NABH Recommended (Entry Level)**: Hospital has met entry-level criteria and is working toward full accreditation
- **Non-NABH Government**: Government hospitals that provide quality care under government oversight but have not undergone NABH accreditation process

### Government Hospitals (Subsidized / Free Treatment)

#### Mahavir Cancer Sansthan and Research Centre (MCSRC), Patna
- **Type:** Dedicated super-specialty cancer hospital, run by Mahavir Mandir Trust
- **Accreditation:** NABH Recommended (Entry Level, Jan 2024)
- **Beds:** 618 beds — Bihar's largest dedicated cancer centre
- **Location:** Phulwarisharif, Patna-801505, Bihar
- **Phone:** +91-612-2250127 / 2253956 / 3222583
- **Services:** Surgical oncology, medical oncology (chemotherapy), radiation oncology, radiology, nuclear medicine, pathology, palliative care
- **Facilities:** Operation theatres, ICU, blood bank, pharmacy, diagnostic imaging
- **Key feature:** Largest dedicated cancer hospital in eastern India. Pediatric patients are treated free. Low-income adult patients receive financial assistance (Rs. 10,000 grant from Mahavir Mandir Trust for poor adult patients).
- **PM-JAY:** Empanelled — cashless treatment available
- **Accommodation:** Affordable dharmashala (guest lodging) near the hospital for outstation patients and companions
- **How to access:** Walk-in OPD registration available (arrive in morning). Bring photo ID and any referral slip. Referral from district hospital helpful but not mandatory.
- **Getting there:** ~7 km from Patna Junction railway station, ~13 km from city bus stand (autos available), ~5 km from Patna airport

#### AIIMS Patna (All India Institute of Medical Sciences)
- **Type:** Central government premier medical institute
- **Accreditation:** Non-NABH (government oversight — AIIMS operates under its own central government quality standards)
- **Location:** Phulwarisharif, Patna-801507, Bihar
- **Phone:** +91-612-2451070
- **Services:** Radiation oncology (modern linear accelerator and brachytherapy unit), surgical oncology, medical oncology, pathology, radiology
- **Specialized clinics:** Head and neck oncology, breast oncology, pediatric oncology
- **Key feature:** Tertiary referral center with multi-disciplinary tumor boards. Provides free cancer screening camps. Advanced care at low cost.
- **PM-JAY:** Empanelled — cashless treatment available
- **How to access:** Register through AIIMS ORS portal (ors.gov.in) or at hospital reception. OPD in Basement, LGB block. Carry Aadhaar, referrals, and PM-JAY/ABHA information.

#### IGIMS (Indira Gandhi Institute of Medical Sciences) / State Cancer Institute, Patna
- **Type:** State government tertiary hospital with dedicated oncology department
- **Accreditation:** Non-NABH (government oversight — operates as State Cancer Institute under NPCDCS)
- **Location:** Sheikhpura, Patna-800014, Bihar
- **Phone:** 0612-2297631, 0612-2297099
- **Services:** Oncology department with chemotherapy and radiation therapy facilities, surgical services, diagnostic services
- **Key feature:** State Cancer Institute established under NPCDCS (National Programme for Prevention and Control of Cancer, Diabetes, CVD and Stroke). Highly subsidized or free cancer treatment for low-income patients.
- **PM-JAY:** Empanelled — cashless treatment available
- **OPD schedule:** Monday–Friday 8:30am–4:30pm, Saturday till noon
- **Registration fee:** Approximately Rs. 50
- **How to access:** Register online (IGIMS app/website) or at the registration counter. Bring government ID (Aadhaar) and any medical records or referrals.

#### Homi Bhabha Cancer Hospital & Research Centre, Muzaffarpur
- **Type:** Tata Memorial Centre (TMC) unit — government cancer hospital
- **Accreditation:** Non-NABH (government oversight — TMC/DAE standards)
- **Location:** Srikrishnanagar, Muzaffarpur, Bihar
- **Services:** Surgical oncology, medical oncology, radiation oncology, pathology, radiology, palliative care
- **Key feature:** Regional cancer center for North Bihar. Operated by TMC (same institution as Tata Memorial Hospital, Mumbai). Provides comprehensive cancer care closer to home for patients from North Bihar, reducing need to travel to Patna or out of state.
- **PM-JAY:** Empanelled — cashless treatment available
- **How to access:** OPD registration at hospital. Referral from district hospital recommended.
- **Getting there:** Muzaffarpur is ~80 km north of Patna. Regular trains and buses from Patna Junction.

#### Rajendra Memorial Research Institute (RMRI), Patna
- **Type:** Government research institute with medical services
- **Services:** Limited oncology referral services
- **Note:** Primarily a research institute; cancer patients are typically referred to MCSRC or IGIMS

- **Phone:** +91-612-7107700

### Choosing Between Government and Private Hospitals

| Factor | Government (MCSRC, AIIMS, IGIMS) | NABH Private (Paras, Medanta, Ruban) |
|---|---|---|
| **Cost** | Free to very low cost | Higher — verify insurance coverage |
| **PM-JAY** | Fully empanelled | Select packages only |
| **Wait times** | Longer (especially OPD) | Generally shorter |
| **Equipment** | Good (linear accelerators, CT/MRI) | Often newer (PET-CT, robotic, BMT) |
| **Financial assistance** | Social welfare desk, trust grants | Limited — check hospital policies |
| **Accreditation** | Government oversight standards | NABH accredited |

**Recommendation for PM-JAY patients:** Start with government hospitals (MCSRC, AIIMS, IGIMS) where cashless treatment is fully available. Private hospitals accept PM-JAY for select packages only and may have additional out-of-pocket costs.

## Typical Patient Journey in Bihar

### Step 1: Initial Suspicion
- Visit local doctor or district hospital
- Get basic investigations (blood tests, imaging)

### Step 2: Referral to Cancer Center
- Get referral letter from local doctor (helpful but not always mandatory)
- Common first stops: Mahavir Cancer Sansthan or IGIMS Patna

### Step 3: Hospital Registration and OPD Visit
- Arrive early (morning) at the hospital
- Register at the OPD desk with photo ID and referral slip
- Attend the appropriate OPD (Medical Oncology, Surgical Oncology, or Radiation Oncology)

### Step 4: Diagnosis and Staging
- Doctor consultation and clinical examination
- Prescribed tests: biopsy, imaging (CT/MRI/PET scan), blood work
- Complete tests within the hospital
- Pathology report (may take 5-10 working days)
- Review results with doctor

### Step 5: Treatment Planning
- Multi-disciplinary team (MDT) / tumor board discussion
- Treatment plan: surgery, chemotherapy, radiation, or combination
- Discussion of costs and financial assistance options
- Visit hospital social welfare desk for PM-JAY and other scheme applications

### Step 6: Treatment
- Treatment as per plan
- Collect medicines from hospital pharmacy or trusted local chemist
- Regular follow-up visits
- Side effect management

### Step 7: Follow-up
- Regular follow-up as advised (typically every 3-6 months initially)
- Surveillance imaging and blood tests
- Survivorship care

## Documents to Carry for Hospital Visit

Essential documents for any cancer care hospital visit in India:
1. **Government ID** (Aadhaar card, voter ID, or passport)
2. **Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY) card or ABHA ID** if available
3. **Previous medical records** (prescriptions, discharge summaries)
4. **Biopsy/pathology reports** (if any)
5. **Imaging CDs/reports** (X-ray, CT, MRI, PET scan)
6. **Blood test reports** (recent, within last 2 weeks)
7. **Referral letter** from local doctor (if available)
8. **Passport-size photographs** (2-3, for hospital registration)
9. **BPL card / income certificate** (for subsidized treatment)
10. **Health insurance documents** (if any private insurance)
11. **Current medication list** with dosages
12. **Water, snacks** for long OPD waits
13. **An organized file/folder** to keep all reports, prescriptions, and appointment slips together

## Caregiver Logistics: Travel and Accommodation in Patna

### Getting to Hospitals
- **Mahavir Cancer Sansthan:** ~7 km from Patna Junction station, ~5 km from Patna airport. Autos and taxis available.
- **AIIMS Patna:** Phulwarisharif area, same vicinity as MCSRC
- **IGIMS:** Sheikhpura area, central Patna
- **Paras HMRI:** Bailey Road area, central Patna (~3 km from Patna Junction)
- **Jay Prabha Medanta:** Kankarbagh area, south Patna
- **Ruban Memorial:** Patliputra Colony, south-west Patna

### Accommodation
- Mahavir Cancer Sansthan has an affordable **dharmashala** (guest lodging) near the hospital
- Budget hotels available near all major hospitals
- Plan for multiple trips — treatment often requires 4-8 cycles of chemotherapy over several months

### OPD Tips
- Arrive 30-60 minutes before scheduled time
- Hospital canteens offer modest meals; nearby eateries also available
- Carry water, snacks, and entertainment for waiting periods
- Keep a file with all previous reports organized chronologically

## Important Notes
- Government hospitals generally offer significantly lower costs than private hospitals
- Many government cancer centers have social work departments that help with financial assistance
- PMJAY (Ayushman Bharat) is accepted at empanelled government and some private hospitals — present your PM-JAY card or Aadhaar at admission for cashless treatment
- Treatment costs vary significantly between government and private facilities
- Waiting times at government hospitals may be longer; plan accordingly
- Always retain all medical bills, prescriptions, and test reports — needed for reimbursement and assistance applications
