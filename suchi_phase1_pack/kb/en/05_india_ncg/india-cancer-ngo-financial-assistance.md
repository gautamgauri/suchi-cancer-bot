## Overview

Several organizations in India provide financial assistance, emotional support, and practical help to cancer patients and their families. Many of these services are free or heavily subsidized.

## National Organizations

### Indian Cancer Society (ICS)
- **Helpline:** 1800-22-1951 (toll-free, 24/7)
- **Services:** Financial assistance for treatment, cancer awareness programs, early detection camps
- **How to access:** Call the helpline or contact through their regional offices
- **Coverage:** Pan-India

### Cancer Patients Aid Association (CPAA)
- **Contact:** 022-24122413, 022-24122396
- **Location:** Mumbai (headquartered)
- **Services:** Financial aid for cancer treatment, free diagnostic camps, emotional support
- **How to access:** Contact directly or through the treating hospital's social work department
- **Coverage:** Primarily Mumbai/Maharashtra, but provides assistance nationally

### CanSupport
- **Contact:** 011-41010539, 011-41010540
- **Location:** Delhi NCR
- **Services:** Free home-based palliative care, emotional support, counseling, caregiver training
- **How to access:** Self-referral or hospital referral
- **Coverage:** Delhi NCR primarily

### Tata Trusts Cancer Care Programme
- **Services:** Supports cancer treatment through Tata Memorial Hospital network
- **How to access:** Through Tata Memorial Hospital or its affiliate centers (including Homi Bhabha Cancer Hospital, Varanasi)
- **Coverage:** Pan-India through hospital network

### Kailash Nath Katju Trust
- **Services:** Financial assistance for cancer treatment
- **How to access:** Through hospital social work department

### Rotary Cancer Trust
- **Services:** Financial support for cancer treatment at select hospitals
- **How to access:** Through Rotary clubs or hospital social work departments

## Bihar-Specific Support

### Hospital Social Work Departments
- **Mahavir Cancer Sansthan, Patna** has a social work department that helps patients navigate financial assistance
- **IGIMS Patna** provides assistance through the hospital administration
- **AIIMS Patna** has patient welfare services

### State Government Support
- **Bihar Chief Minister's Treatment Scheme:** Financial assistance for serious illnesses
- **State Illness Assistance Fund:** For BPL families
- Contact: District Magistrate's office or Block Development Officer

### Local Support Groups
- Cancer support groups exist in Patna and other cities in Bihar
- Ask the hospital social worker for connections to local groups
- Online support groups are also available in Hindi and English

## How to Apply for Financial Assistance

### General Process
1. **Get a cost estimate** from the treating hospital
2. **Collect required documents:**
   - Medical records and treatment plan
   - Income certificate or BPL card
   - Government ID (Aadhaar, voter ID)
   - Bank account details
   - Hospital registration number
3. **Contact the hospital social worker** - they know which schemes and NGOs to apply to
4. **Apply to multiple sources** - most organizations encourage applying to multiple schemes
5. **Keep copies of all applications and correspondence**

### Tips for Successful Applications
- Apply as early as possible (ideally at diagnosis, before treatment starts)
- Be honest about your financial situation
- Provide all requested documents
- Follow up regularly
- Ask the social worker to help with the application

## Emotional and Practical Support

### Counseling Services
- **iCall (TISS):** 9152987821 (Mon-Sat, 8am-10pm IST) - psychosocial counseling
- **Vandrevala Foundation:** 9999666555 (24/7) - mental health support
- **Muktaa Charitable Foundation:** 7887889882 (Mon-Sat)

### Practical Support
- **Hospital social workers** can help with:
  - Connecting with NGOs
  - Arranging accommodation for outstation patients
  - Navigating government paperwork
  - Providing emotional counseling referrals
  - Organizing support group connections

### Support for Caregivers
- Caregiver burnout is real and common
- Many of the above organizations also support caregivers
- Ask the hospital for caregiver support groups
- iCall (TISS) provides counseling for caregivers as well
