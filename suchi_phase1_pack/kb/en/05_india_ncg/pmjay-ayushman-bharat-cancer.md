## What is Ayushman Bharat - Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (PM-JAY)?

Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY is the Government of India's flagship health insurance scheme. It provides health coverage of up to Rs. 5 lakh per family per year for secondary and tertiary care hospitalization. The scheme targets economically vulnerable families identified through the Socio-Economic Caste Census (SECC) 2011 data.

## Cancer Treatment Under PM-JAY

PM-JAY covers cancer treatment including:
- **Surgery** for cancer (various types)
- **Chemotherapy** (multiple regimens covered)
- **Radiation therapy** (external beam, brachytherapy)
- **Diagnostic procedures** (biopsy, imaging for cancer staging)
- **Hospitalization** related to cancer treatment
- **Follow-up care** within the package

### What is covered
- Up to Rs. 5,00,000 (5 lakh) per family per year
- Pre-hospitalization expenses (up to 3 days before admission)
- Post-hospitalization expenses (up to 15 days after discharge)
- Treatment is cashless at empanelled hospitals

### What is NOT covered
- OPD (outpatient) consultations (only inpatient/day-care)
- Cosmetic procedures
- Organ transplant (separate scheme)
- Individual treatment costs exceeding package rates
- Fertility treatments

## How to Check Eligibility

Eligibility is based on socio-economic criteria determined by the state, targeting poor families (BPL/AAY or state-determined criteria).

### Online
1. Visit the PM-JAY website or the mera.pmjay.gov.in portal
2. Enter your mobile number or Aadhaar number or ration card number
3. Check if your family is listed

### Offline
1. Visit your nearest **Ayushman Mitra** at a government hospital
2. Visit the **Common Service Centre (CSC)** in your area
3. Call **14555** (PM-JAY helpline, toll-free)
4. Visit the **District PM-JAY office**

## How to Get the Ayushman Card

### If already eligible (listed in SECC data):
1. Visit an empanelled hospital or CSC with your Aadhaar card
2. The Ayushman Mitra will verify your identity
3. E-card is generated on the spot (free of cost)
4. You can also download it from the PM-JAY app

### If not listed but eligible under state expansion:
- Some states have expanded eligibility criteria
- In **Bihar**, additional categories may be covered under state health schemes
- Contact the district health office or call 14555 for state-specific eligibility

## Using PM-JAY for Cancer Treatment - Step by Step

### Step 1: Verify eligibility
- Check at hospital Ayushman Mitra desk or call 14555
- Keep Aadhaar card and any government ID ready

### Step 2: Get your Ayushman card
- Generated at the hospital or CSC
- No cost for card generation
- Family members listed under the same household are covered

### Step 3: Hospital selection
- Choose an empanelled hospital (government or private)
- Ask the Ayushman Mitra desk for the list of empanelled hospitals
- Many government cancer centers are empanelled (e.g., Mahavir Cancer Sansthan, Tata Memorial Hospital)

### Step 4: Admission and treatment
- Show your Ayushman card or Aadhaar at the hospital's Ayushman counter
- The hospital will verify and create a pre-authorization request
- Request pre-authorization for the required treatment (hospital staff will assist)
- Ensure all hospital bills and forms are documented under PM-JAY to avoid out-of-pocket payment
- Treatment is cashless up to the package rate — no upfront payment required for covered treatment packages
- You should not be asked to pay for covered procedures

### Step 5: If costs exceed Rs. 5 lakh
- Additional costs beyond the annual limit must be borne by the patient
- Ask the hospital social work department about charitable funds
- Some hospitals have trust funds for patients who exhaust PM-JAY limits
- Government cancer centers may provide additional subsidies

## PM-JAY Helpline and Grievance

- **Toll-free helpline:** 14555
- **Grievance portal:** Available on PM-JAY website
- **Ayushman Mitra:** Available at empanelled hospital help desks
- If a hospital refuses PM-JAY despite being empanelled, file a complaint at 14555

### Bihar State Health Scheme
- Bihar has additional state health coverage programs
- Check with the district health office for state-specific schemes
- Some schemes cover transport costs for patients traveling to tertiary centers

### Accessing PM-JAY in Bihar

- CSCs are available in most blocks and panchayats
- District hospitals can verify eligibility and generate cards

## Other Government Schemes for Cancer Patients

### Rashtriya Arogya Nidhi (RAN)
- Financial assistance for BPL patients for treatment at government hospitals
- Covers treatment costs at super-specialty hospitals
- Apply through the hospital's medical superintendent

### Health Minister's Discretionary Grant (HMDG)
- For patients seeking treatment at government hospitals
- Provides financial assistance up to Rs. 1.5 lakh per case
- Apply through the hospital administration

### Prime Minister's National Relief Fund (PMNRF)
- Financial assistance for medical treatment
- Apply directly to the PM's office with medical documents

### State-Level Schemes
- **Bihar Chief Minister's Treatment Scheme**: Covers treatment for serious illnesses
- **State Illness Assistance Fund**: Available for BPL families
- Contact the district magistrate's office or district health office for details

## Tips for Cancer Patients Using Government Schemes

1. **Apply early** - Don't wait until treatment starts; check eligibility and get your card at diagnosis
2. **Keep all documents** - Maintain copies of all medical records, bills, and correspondence
3. **Ask the hospital social worker** - Most government cancer centers have social work departments that help navigate schemes
4. **Know your rights** - Empanelled hospitals cannot refuse PM-JAY patients for covered procedures
5. **Multiple schemes possible** - You may be eligible for more than one scheme; ask the social worker
6. **Transport costs** - Some schemes cover transport; ask specifically about this
7. **Medicines** - If hospital doesn't have medicines in stock, the social work department may help source them
8. **Companion support** - Some hospitals provide free or subsidized accommodation for patient companions
