# Suchi Voice Guide

**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 2026-05-09  
**Applies to:** All distribution channels (LinkedIn, Twitter, Instagram, WhatsApp, YouTube Short)

---

## What Suchi sounds like

> A calm public health educator. A thoughtful oncologist. A trusted caregiver guide.

Not a startup growth team. Not influencer healthcare. Not SEO spam medicine.

In a landscape where Indian health content online is chaotic, sensational, or misleading, Suchi's restraint is itself a differentiator. Calm clarity builds trust over time — especially with doctors, caregivers, institutions, and CSR funders.

---

## The Content Formula

```
Human Observation
      +
Medical Clarity
      +
Actionable Guidance
      +
Calm Tone
```

Not:

```
Shock + Fear + Urgency
```

## Before / After Examples

### Opening hooks

❌ **Institutional / generic**
> "Oral cancer is a serious health concern, especially in India."

✓ **Suchi voice**
> "Many people ignore mouth ulcers for weeks, assuming they will heal on their own. Sometimes, they don't."

---

❌ **Disease-definition opener**
> "Oral cancer, a type of head and neck cancer, can affect the lips, tongue, and gums."

✓ **Suchi voice**
> "In Bihar, many people ignore mouth sores for months assuming it's just heat or a minor infection. Sometimes, it turns out to be oral cancer."

---

### Symptom framing

❌ **Fear without relief**
> "These symptoms could mean cancer."

✓ **Suchi voice**
> "While these symptoms may be due to other causes, persistent issues should always be evaluated by a doctor."

---

### Uncertainty

❌ **Overclaims**
> "If you have these symptoms, you may have cancer."

✓ **Suchi voice**
> "These signs aren't always serious — but if they last more than 2–3 weeks, they deserve a doctor's attention."

---

### Actionability

❌ **Vague**
> "Oral cancer is treatable if caught early."

✓ **Suchi voice**
> "If you notice a sore in your mouth that hasn't healed in 2–3 weeks, visit a doctor or dentist. A simple check-up can make a significant difference."

## What to Avoid

| Pattern               | Why                                              |
|-----------------------|--------------------------------------------------|
| "Silent killer"       | Fearbait — creates panic, not action             |
| "Deadly"              | Same — alarm without guidance                    |
| "You may have cancer" | Implied diagnosis — causes unnecessary distress  |
| Miracle narratives    | "Cancer warrior", "beat cancer" — oversimplifies |
| Generic openers       | "Cancer is serious" — wastes the first line      |
| Brochure language     | Accurate but forgettable — no human texture      |

---

## India-Grounding Checklist

Good Suchi content does at least one of these:
- Names local tobacco products (gutka, paan masala, khaini, bidi) instead of just "tobacco"
- Mentions PM-JAY / Ayushman Bharat for affordability context where relevant
- References Bihar, Eastern India, or a recognisable local setting
- Uses local phrasing ("speak to a doctor" feels warmer than "seek medical attention")

---

## Optimisation Target

> **Maximum Useful Attention** — not Maximum Engagement.

The goal is not CTR or virality. The goal is that the right person, in the right moment of anxiety or uncertainty, finds calm, accurate, actionable guidance — and feels trusted enough to act on it.

That trust, compounded over time, is the Suchi brand moat.
