### Important Notes on Costs

- Costs at **government hospitals** (AIIMS, state cancer centers, regional cancer centers) are significantly lower because medicines and services are subsidized
- **Private hospital costs** vary widely; corporate hospital chains tend to be at the higher end
- **Immunotherapy and targeted therapy** are the most expensive treatments, often Rs 1-4 lakh per cycle
- **Ongoing costs** (follow-up visits, blood tests, supportive medications) add Rs 50,000 - 2,00,000 per year
- Costs do NOT include travel, accommodation, lost wages, or caregiver expenses
- Always ask for a detailed cost estimate before starting treatment

## Financial Aid Programs

### 1. Ayushman Bharat - Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (PM-JAY)

India's largest government health insurance scheme covering cancer treatment.

- **Coverage:** Up to Rs 5,00,000 per family per year for hospitalization
- **What it covers for cancer:** Surgery, chemotherapy, radiation therapy, diagnostics, hospitalization, follow-up care
- **Helpline:** **14555** (toll-free) or **1800-111-565**
- **Check eligibility online:** https://mera.pmjay.gov.in
- **Who is eligible:** Economically vulnerable families identified through SECC 2011 data; all senior citizens aged 70 and above (added September 2024)
- **Empanelled hospitals:** Available at government and select private hospitals across India
- **No premium required:** The scheme is fully government-funded for eligible families

**How to apply for Ayushman Bharat (step by step):**
1. Check eligibility: Call **14555** or visit https://mera.pmjay.gov.in with your Aadhaar number
2. Visit an empanelled hospital's **Ayushman Mitra desk** or a **Common Service Centre (CSC)** near you
3. Carry: Aadhaar card, ration card, any BPL/Antyodaya card, and a photo ID
4. The Ayushman Mitra will verify eligibility and help create your **e-card**
5. Once verified, you receive a PM-JAY e-card (can also be verified via Aadhaar at the hospital)
6. At the hospital, present your e-card or Aadhaar at the **Ayushman counter** before admission
7. Hospital staff will handle **pre-authorization** for treatment — no upfront payment required
8. If authorization is denied or delayed, call **14555** immediately for assistance
9. Keep all hospital documents, bills, and treatment records for your file

### 2. Other Central Government Schemes

- **Rashtriya Arogya Nidhi (RAN):** Financial assistance for BPL patients treated at government super-specialty hospitals. Apply through the hospital Medical Social Worker. Covers up to Rs 15,00,000.
- **Health Minister's Discretionary Grant (HMDG):** Up to Rs 1,50,000 per case for treatment at government hospitals. Apply through hospital social worker.
- **Prime Minister's National Relief Fund (PMNRF):** Financial assistance for medical treatment of serious illness. Apply at https://pmnrf.gov.in or write to PM's office.
- **Central Government Health Scheme (CGHS):** For central government employees and pensioners. Covers cancer treatment at CGHS-empanelled hospitals including chemotherapy, radiation, and surgery. Coverage up to Rs 5,00,000.
- **Jan Aushadhi Kendras:** Government generic medicine stores offering cancer drugs at 50-90% lower prices than branded versions. Find nearest store at http://janaushadhi.gov.in

### 5. Pharmaceutical Patient Assistance Programs

These programs provide free or heavily discounted cancer medicines:

- **Novartis / Max Foundation - CancerPath to Care:** Free imatinib (Glivec) for CML and GIST patients who cannot afford treatment. Also covers breast cancer and other rare cancers. Apply through your treating oncologist or contact The Max Foundation.
- **Roche India - Blue Tree Programme:** Assists cancer, haemophilia, and rare illness patients with access to Roche medicines throughout their treatment journey. Ask your oncologist to apply.
- **Cipla Palliative Care:** Provides free or subsidized pain medications and palliative care drugs.
- **Generic alternatives:** Ask your doctor about generic versions of cancer drugs, which can be 50-90% cheaper. Indian pharma companies (Cipla, Sun Pharma, Natco) manufacture affordable generics of many cancer drugs.

### 6. Crowdfunding Platforms

When insurance and government schemes are not enough, crowdfunding can help bridge the gap:

- **Ketto** (www.ketto.org): India's largest crowdfunding platform for medical emergencies including cancer
- **Milaap** (www.milaap.org): Raised over Rs 2,400 crore across 900,000+ campaigns
- **ImpactGuru** (www.impactguru.com): Top 100 cancer campaigns raised an average of Rs 10 lakh each in FY24

**Tips for successful crowdfunding:**
- Include detailed medical reports and cost estimates
- Share your story with photos (with patient's consent)
- Share the campaign link on WhatsApp groups, social media
- Update donors regularly on treatment progress
- Crowdfunding is legal in India; donations may qualify for 80G tax deduction

## Bihar-Specific Cancer Resources (Priority)

### Cancer Treatment Facilities in Patna

1. **Mahavir Cancer Sansthan (MCSRC)** - Phulwarisharif, Patna
   - Bihar's only super-specialty comprehensive cancer centre (618 beds)
   - Departments: Surgical oncology, medical oncology, radiation oncology, nuclear medicine
   - **Financial aid:** Free treatment for pediatric patients; Rs 10,000 grant for poor adult patients (Mahavir Mandir Trust)
   - Accepts Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY)
   - Phone: +91-612-2250127 / 2253956 / 3222583

2. **AIIMS Patna** - Phulwarisharif, Patna
   - Government tertiary hospital with radiation oncology (linear accelerator, brachytherapy)
   - Specialized clinics: head and neck, breast, pediatric oncology
   - Free cancer screening camps
   - Very low cost treatment
   - Phone: +91-612-2451070

3. **IGIMS (State Cancer Institute)** - Sheikhpura, Patna
   - State government tertiary hospital with dedicated oncology
   - Chemotherapy and radiation therapy
   - Highly subsidized or free treatment for low-income patients
   - OPD fee: approximately Rs 50
   - Phone: 0612-2297631 / 0612-2297099

4. **PMCH (Patna Medical College and Hospital)** - Patna
   - Government medical college hospital with oncology department
   - Very affordable cancer diagnostics and treatment

### Bihar Financial Assistance

- **Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY):** Available at empanelled hospitals including Mahavir Cancer Sansthan, AIIMS Patna, and IGIMS. Call **14555** to check eligibility.
- **Mukhyamantri Chikitsa Sahayata Yojana:** State scheme providing financial assistance up to Rs 1,00,000 for cancer treatment. Apply through District Magistrate office.
- **State Illness Assistance Fund:** Additional fund for treatment at government hospitals. Apply through hospital social worker.
- **Mahavir Mandir Trust:** Provides direct financial assistance to poor cancer patients at Mahavir Cancer Sansthan. Pediatric cancer patients treated free of charge.
- **PMNRF / HMDG:** Can be applied for through hospital social workers at AIIMS and IGIMS.

### How to Get Started (for Bihar patients)

1. Visit the nearest cancer centre OPD (Mahavir, AIIMS, or IGIMS in Patna)
2. Register at OPD desk with Aadhaar card and any referral documents
3. Ask to meet the **Medical Social Worker** at the hospital - they help with all financial scheme applications
4. Check PM-JAY eligibility immediately (call **14555** or visit Ayushman Mitra desk at hospital)
5. Bring: Aadhaar card, ration card, income certificate, BPL card (if available), all medical records
6. Apply for multiple schemes simultaneously - do not wait for one before applying for others

## Important Reminders

- **Costs are approximate** and vary significantly by hospital, city, stage of cancer, and individual treatment plan
- **Always ask for a detailed cost estimate** before starting treatment
- **Government hospitals** offer the same quality of cancer treatment at much lower costs
- **Multiple financial schemes can be used together** - apply to all that you are eligible for
- **Hospital social workers** are your best starting point for navigating financial assistance
- **Generic medicines** can reduce drug costs by 50-90% - always ask your doctor about generic options
- **Keep copies** of all bills, prescriptions, medical records, and scheme applications
